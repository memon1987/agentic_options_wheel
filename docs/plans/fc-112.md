## Context

The wheel's daily credit-only roller (FC-078) fires when `close / strike ≥ rolling.itm_trigger_ratio`, and the wheel ships `0.98` (`config/settings.yaml:214`). A 0.15–0.25-delta short-dated call is born at 97–99 % of its strike, so at 0.98 the roller can re-write the engine's own call the day it was sold — buy back at the ask, sell a higher strike up to 14 days further out at any delta ≤ 0.60 — bypassing every entry gate. The covered-call profile was set to `1.00` (FC-100 DD-1, operator decision D-A, 2026-09-08: true-ITM-only defence), and the wheel's 0.98 was left **pending this study**. The operator's principle (2026-09-11, FC-117) is that the two profiles run the *same underlying mechanisms* unless a measured reason says otherwise — so a difference that this study cannot justify should close.

FC-116 (merged `1745f00`, 2026-09-11) supplies the number: roll legs now fill at the placed limits against the modeled book, and every `scenario_runs` row carries `rolls_executed`, `itm_rolls`, `otm_roll_outs`, `roll_net_credit`, `itm_roll_credit`, `otm_roll_out_credit`, `failed_roll_btc_debit`, `roll_legs_resting`, `roll_legs_marketable`, `roll_skips`, `roll_fill_mode`. The ITM/OTM split is `close/old_strike ≥ 1.0 → itm_defence, else otm_roll_out` — so **every roll a 0.98 trigger executes that a 1.00 trigger would not is, by construction, an `otm_roll_out`.**

Three facts fall out of that table and shape the plan:

1. **OTM roll-outs are the majority of what the wheel roller does at 0.98**: 22 of the 32 fit-window rolls (69 %) and $2,824 of $4,595 roll credit (61 %). The study is not about an edge case.
2. **`no_credit_candidate` is the dominant skip** (22 on AAPL against 5 executed rolls). A same-day roll-out at 0.98 with no tenor to sell into rarely clears the credit invariant: **the truncation suppresses exactly the behaviour under test, on the arm under test.** The live IWM rolls of 09-04 and 09-08 extended tenor; the replay's cannot.
3. The effective universe is **7 symbols** (AAPL, AMD, AMZN, GOOGL, IWM, NVDA, UNH); SPY/QQQ sit above `max_stock_price: 400`, F/PFE/KMI/VZ never clear the $0.50 put floor, MSFT is `low_activity`. The 90-day holdout measures three cells. Any decision rule that leans on the holdout alone decides on n = 3.

## Goals

1. Decide, on measured evidence and a rule written down **before** the numbers are read, whether the wheel's `rolling.itm_trigger_ratio` moves 0.98 → 1.00 (same mechanism as the covered-call profile) or stays.
2. Make the replay's wheel roller see the ladder the live roller sees (the 7 + 14 reach) **before** the study runs — otherwise the study measures the truncation.
3. Name and neutralise (or bound and label) every bias that could decide the answer by itself: the 2.46× modeled spread, the resting-leg residual, the reach, the premium floor, coverage, the thin holdout.
4. Leave a re-runnable read (`tools/diagnostics/`) and a written record (`docs/investigations/`) an operator can read the verdict from, plus a rolling weekly series of the same comparison so the verdict is re-checked, not frozen.

## Non-goals

- **No production trading-config change in this FC.** `config/settings.yaml`, `config/covered_call.yaml`, `src/strategy/call_roller.py`, the bot services and their env are untouched. The study produces a recommendation; the flip (if any) is a new FC with its own PR, `Config` census test (FC-100 T-1's "exactly one key differs" assertion inverts to "no key differs"), alert-twin check and FC-078 amendment.
- Not a study of the roller's other knobs (`min_net_credit_per_contract`, `max_replacement_delta`, `max_extension_days`, imminence) — the FC-100 trader's note about a $0.10 credit on a 50-delta buy-back at exactly 1.00 is real and is **not** this FC (§Open questions OQ-6).
- No engine fill-model change beyond the reach (FC-116 owns the fill rule). No new `scenario_runs` columns. No console feature: the console already renders `delta_vs_base_annualized` per arm (`CompareView.tsx`) and the sweep markdown prints `### Roll activity and credit`; the study reads BigQuery.
- Not a decision about whether the wheel should roll at all. `rolling.enabled: false` is a **control** arm; if it wins outright that is a separate finding and a separate FC.

## Design decisions, with rejected alternatives (made — do not relitigate in the build)

### DD-1 — The question, the metric and the decision rule, fixed before the data is read

**Question.** Over the wheel's live universe, trailing year with a 90-day holdout, does replacing `rolling.itm_trigger_ratio: 0.98` with `1.00` — everything else identical — change the wheel's return, and in which direction?

**Primary contrast (the only one that decides):** arm `t100` (`rolling.itm_trigger_ratio: 1.00`, `roll_fill_mode: limit`) vs the implicit `base` (0.98, `limit`), FIT window, per symbol, `Δann = annualized_return(t100) − annualized_return(base)`, on symbols where **both** cells are `measured`. Summary statistics: `N+` / `N−` / `N0` (sign counts), `median(Δann)`. The FIT window is primary because it is the window that measures (7 symbols vs the holdout's 3); the holdout is a check, below.

**Minimum effect.** `median |Δann| ≥ 0.25 pp` on `$100,000` starting cash — $250/yr, roughly the credit of ONE roll on these rows ($100–$300). An effect smaller than one roll's credit is one roll's timing, not a trigger effect. (OQ-3 lets the operator move this number *before* the read; it may not move after.)

**Verdict rule** (`M` = measurable symbols, both arms measured, expected 6–7):

| verdict | condition |
|---|---|
| **MOVE → 1.00** | `N+ ≥ ⌈5M/7⌉` AND `median(Δann) ≥ +0.25 pp` under `limit`; AND the haircut bracket (DD-3) does not reverse: `N+ ≥ ⌈4M/7⌉` and `median(Δann) ≥ 0` under `haircut`; AND the holdout does not refute (sign agreement on the report's own column ≥ 50 % when `comparable ≥ 4`; with `comparable < 4` the holdout is declared *uninformative*, stated, and does not block) |
| **KEEP 0.98** | the mirror image: `N− ≥ ⌈5M/7⌉` AND `median(Δann) ≤ −0.25 pp` under `limit`, bracket not reversed, holdout not refuting |
| **MIXED** | anything else |

**Structural validity checks (the run is void, not MIXED, if any fails):** every `t100` row has `otm_roll_outs == 0` (at 1.00 the OTM bucket is empty by construction — the same "non-zero is a finding" rule the CC replay applies); every row has `roll_fill_mode` equal to its arm's declared mode; `rolls_executed == itm_rolls + otm_roll_outs`; the `t100` arm differs from `base` on at least one roll-bearing row (a placebo arm cannot decide anything — the FC-096-a gate, applied here).

**What MIXED resolves to — pre-registered tie-break (OQ-1, BLOCKING, operator).** Recommendation: **MIXED → 1.00**, because (i) the operator's stated principle is that the profiles share mechanisms unless a measured reason says otherwise, and "no measurable difference" is not a reason; (ii) the qualitative case against 0.98 (bypassing every entry gate on the engine's own call) stands whether or not it shows in a year of returns. The alternative, MIXED → keep 0.98 (status quo, the live-evidenced behaviour), is defensible and is the operator's call; the plan records whichever is chosen and the tool applies it. Either way, a MIXED verdict also keeps the pins running four more Saturdays and re-applies the rule to the 4-week medians before FC-112 closes.

**Reported but non-deciding (must be in the record):** the same contrast on `total_return`, `assignment_rate`, `cycles_completed`, `calls_sold`, `max_drawdown`; the roll economics per arm — `rolls_executed`, `itm_rolls`, `otm_roll_outs`, `roll_net_credit` split, `failed_roll_btc_debit`, `roll_legs_resting / (resting + marketable)`, `roll_skips` by reason; the `noroll` control's Δ vs base; the `t099` dose-response arm's Δ (one-shot only, DD-7).

*Rejected:* deciding on `roll_net_credit` (a 1.00 trigger has less roll credit by construction — it rolls less; credit is a cost/benefit input, not the outcome); deciding on the holdout sign-agreement column alone (n = 3 today); a pooled portfolio number (the sweep's own guardrail: the per-symbol grid always renders, there is no blended mode); `annualized_return_on_collateral` (the same ranking on this sizer; the standard column is what the standing series trends).

### DD-2 — PR-1: the wheel replay's roll reach becomes the roll horizon (7 + 14 = 21), a deliberate re-baseline, BEFORE the study

`roll_horizon_reach` (`src/backtesting/scenarios/runner.py:534-572`) returns 0 for the wheel by design (FC-096 Phase C H3, "belongs to FC-112"). PR-1 removes that short-circuit: the wheel's horizon is `call_target_dte (7) + rolling.max_extension_days (14) = 21 = MAX_SWEEPABLE_DTE`, so — unlike the covered-call profile's 28-vs-21 — **the wheel has no residual truncation after PR-1**. The keep-conditions stay: 0 when rolling is off, 0 when the extension is ≤ 0.

Why it is a precondition and not a labelled bias: §Context fact 2 — the truncation removes the tenor a 0.98 roll-out sells into, so it biases *against* the arm under test and would flatter `t100` (which rolls later, with less of the old tenor left, and so loses less of the ladder). A study run at reach 7 measures the replay's truncation, not the trigger. Entry selection is unaffected by construction (`_check_call_criteria_detailed` caps entries at `call_target_dte`; the put scanner at `put_target_dte`), and PR-1 pins that with a test.

Consequences, all accepted: every stored wheel roll number moves once (a golden re-baseline in the FC-116 style, with the before/after table in the PR body); `ENGINE_VERSION → fc-112-wheel-roll-reach` in the three byte-pinned copies (the FC-116 rule: a merge that changes what a number *means* bumps the version, and a roll count over a full ladder means something different); the engine identity moves (it moves on any `src/**` change anyway); the weekly wheel series steps on roll-bearing rows at that version boundary — the trend query already partitions on `engine_version`; `DTE_REACH_BIAS` now applies to every wheel run's *roll candidates* (thin prints past 7 DTE), so its wording must say which leg it applies to. Materialisation cost: the lake stores `universe_dte = 22` and the wheel items already local-hit `@22` objects (FC-117 DD-2), so no vendor cost; replay per cell grows with the chain — measured on the first Saturday.

*Rejected:* (a) run at reach 7 and label — the label would say "the arm under test could not do the thing it does live"; (b) widen via a `strategy.call_target_dte: 21` arm — changes entry selection, which is not the question; (c) fold into FC-116 — decided no there (Q4, default no; "a separate S PR immediately after, before FC-112 runs"); (d) widen to 28 — the lake stops at 22 and asking past it makes an arm read "nothing qualified" (`overrides.py` DTE rule).

### DD-3 — The 2.46× spread bias is bracketed with a second fill mode, not estimated

The modeled half-spread is 2.46× the real RTH spread (`docs/BACKTEST_ENGINE.md` §Every known bias). Under `roll_fill_mode: limit` a completed roll pays the full modeled spread twice (`−0.75·(hs_old + hs_new)` per share vs the old haircut fill; base mode both legs marketable). The arm that rolls **more** pays that penalty more often — and 0.98 rolls more (22 of 32 fit rolls are OTM roll-outs). Left alone, the spread model could produce a MOVE verdict on its own.

Bracket: every trigger arm is run under **both** fill modes. Per completed roll, the crossing cost per share is `2·hs_model` under `limit`, `0.5·hs_model` under `haircut` (`mid ∓ 0.25·hs`), and about `2·hs_model/2.46 ≈ 0.81·hs_model` at the REAL spread — i.e. the live number sits *inside* the bracket, nearer the haircut end. The verdict is required to hold at both ends (DD-1); a verdict that flips between the two modes was decided by the spread model and is MIXED. `roll_fill_mode` is an arm field (FC-116 D2), so this costs two extra arms and no code.

*Rejected:* recovering `hs` per leg from the artifact ledger and de-biasing the credit — the ledger stores `fill`, `limit_price`, `fill_rule`, not the book, and a corrected number would be a third fill model nobody reviewed; a single mode plus a footer label — the label cannot say which way the verdict would have gone.

### DD-4 — The instrument is two rolling pins; a one-shot sim-service submission is the optional early read, same bodies

Pins (`POST /api/v2/sims/pins`) store the window SHAPE and the battery re-anchors it every Saturday (FC-096 B4 rev 2), so a pinned comparison is a **rolling series of the same contrast** — one point per week, `submitted_via='battery'`, on the same day and lake as the standing wheel set. The verdict is read from the **first Saturday point after PR-1 deploys**; the next three points are the monitor (DD-1 MIXED path; a sign flip of the primary contrast within those weeks re-opens the read). Why not the console/sim-service as the source of truth: a one-shot answers once, on one window, and the operator's console session cannot be re-run by a cron. Why not pins only: the operator may want the answer before Saturday — the same two spec bodies may be POSTed to `/api/v2/sims/run` (≤ 12 symbols, ≤ 240 cells, `submitted_via='sim-service'`); the record cites the battery row, and a one-shot that disagrees with the first Saturday point on the verdict is itself MIXED (a one-week window shift flipped it).

Pin shape, driven by the battery's `BATTERY_MAX_PIN_CELLS = 60` and the API's 12-symbol cap: **pin-A** `{t100, noroll}` + implicit base = 3 arms × 7 symbols × 2 splits = 42 cells; **pin-B** `{base_haircut, t100_haircut}` + base = 42 cells. Two of the 20 active slots (one is used today: the GOOGL proof pin `a2e72ff8bb024730`). Wall cost ≈ 2 × (7 local-hit materialisations + 42 replays) ≈ 3–5 min after the standing sets, inside the cap.

*Rejected:* one pin with all five arms (84 cells > 60, refused by the battery); per-symbol pins (14 slots for one question); adding the arms to the standing wheel set (the standing set is a byte contract, FC-117 DD-5).

### DD-5 — Universe: the pins carry the seven symbols that measure; the study run may carry all fourteen

The seven others cannot produce a `measured` cell on this window for structural reasons (§Context fact 3), and 7 × 6 empty cells per pin is 42 cells of the cap spent on `insufficient`. The one-shot may include them (a third submission of the seven, cheap) so the record can say "all fourteen, seven measurable" from data rather than from this plan. If a later week moves a symbol into the measurable set (a price-band or floor change), the pins are re-created — a pin's symbol list is part of its identity.

### DD-6 — Which cells feed which number

Return contrasts use `measured` cells only, both arms (the report's own rule: never average `insufficient` / `low_activity` / `error`). Roll economics are read on **every non-errored cell** — a roll is a fact whether or not a cycle completed (AMZN/GOOGL holdout: 7 rolls each, `insufficient`). Pin rows are selected by `pin_id`; standing rows by `pin_id IS NULL` (the 09-11 battery already holds the GOOGL proof pin's rows beside the standing GOOGL rows — a per-window aggregate that forgets this double-counts GOOGL, §Found 1).

### DD-7 — Controls: `noroll` always; `t099` in the one-shot only

`noroll` (`rolling.enabled: false`; allowlisted, and neither the sim-service nor the `data-backfill` Job exports `ROLLER_ENABLED`, so it is not env-shadowed) is the control that says whether the roller adds anything at either trigger. `t099` (0.99) is a dose-response check — the trigger should move the OTM count monotonically 0.98 → 0.99 → 1.00 — worth one arm in the one-shot, not a fourth pin.

### DD-8 — Nothing in production trading config moves here

The FC entry says "decision recorded as an FC-078 amendment either way". This plan's output is the verdict, the record and the amendment; the flip is its own FC (§Non-goals). The PR-1 `git diff --stat` must show no `config/`, `src/strategy/`, `deploy/` (other than none) lines — T-9 pins it.

### DD-9 — Identity and dedup, stated

PR-1 moves `engine_identity` and `ENGINE_VERSION`; every stored `sweep_key` misses once; the next Saturday replays the standing sets and every active pin under the new identity (as it does every week — the standing sets re-anchor). The two new pins are created **after** PR-1 is deployed, so their first point is on the full ladder. A one-shot run on day D and the Saturday pin point on day S have different `window_end`s, hence different keys — no dedup between them, by design. The pins' `base` arm duplicates the standing wheel item's base at a different key (`scenarios` differ) — a ~1 s replay per cell, accepted.
