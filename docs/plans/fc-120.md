## Context

The covered-call roller (FC-078, live on the CC service since 2026-09-17 via FC-100) prices its buy-to-close at `round(ask, 2)` (`evaluate_roll_opportunity`) and its sell-to-open at `round(bid, 2)` (`_stc_limit_from_quote`) from `AlpacaClient.get_option_quote`, polls 120 s (`_poll_order_fill`), then cancels (`_cancel_and_settle`). This plan establishes, from the full record on both services, what can and cannot be said about why — then specifies the instrumentation that makes the next miss diagnosable (PR-1, **live since 2026-10-02**) and the pricing/execution rule that should stop most of them (PR-2, amended rev 5).

**Five facts the plan rests on (rev 5 wording; fact 1 is now history, fact 5 is corrected):**

1. **Until PR-1 the log carried no quote.** Pre-10-02 `call_roll_btc_placed` / `call_roll_stc_placed` logged `limit_price`, `contracts`, `pricing_mode` (BTC only) — not bid, ask, stamp, feed or sizes; `get_option_quote` returned all of them and the roller dropped them. Quote age at placement is unknowable for every pre-PR-1 event above. Since `f4f12fa` every leg row carries the DD-2 set (`_placement_fields`, `_reread_fields`, `_post_settle_quote`, `_log_leg_settled`).
2. **The feed is Alpaca's `indicative` feed** on both services (`alpaca_client.OPTION_QUOTE_FEED`, passed explicitly by `get_option_quote`; the OPRA agreement is unsigned — FC-072 §Rollout 0, `docs/CLAUDE.md` §Trading APIs / §Real-money gates). Alpaca's description: *"trades are delayed and quotes are modified."* The endpoint is `get_option_latest_quote` — the latest quote, not a snapshot. The stock side is IEX, real-time (`AlpacaClient.get_stock_quote`, with its own `timestamp`).
3. **Off-grid limits are the fingerprint of a modified quote, not the miss mechanism.** 15 of 29 BTC limits and 12 of 21 rung-1 limits were off the tick grid; an NBBO price cannot be, so those quotes were not NBBO prices. But on-grid misses exist (BTC: AAPL 1.39 08-27, IWM 0.68 09-02; rung 1: five of the nine misses) and off-grid limits filled 10/15 — the grid does not decide the fill. **Every one of the 42 fills was on the grid; 40 were strictly through their limit and two (08-26 AAPL 1.60, 09-28 NVDA 2.01) filled at it after resting 53 s and 24 s**: the placed limit is the binding price only when it sits exactly at the true far side; otherwise fills happen at the NBBO. Live consequence of an off-grid limit: Alpaca *"will attempt to route the order to the NOM. If they are not able to route the order to NOM, the order will be rejected"* (support article "Options Pricing Increments and Options Order Handling") — the same rule `limit_pricing.round_to_tick`'s docstring and FC-088 state. Paper accepted all of them; FC-072 added `round_to_tick` for the entry legs; the roller never calls it. PR-1 now logs `limit_on_tick` on every leg: false on all four post-PR-1 placements above $3.
4. **Alpaca paper fills against the real NBBO** (paper-trading docs: matched against the NBBO; a buy limit fills only when `limit ≥ best ask`). So a BTC at the indicative ask fills only when the true ask is at or below it; the 21 fills say the indicative ask sat 0.00–0.07 *above* the true ask; the seven real-market misses say it sat *below* for 120 s. For the six pre-PR-1 misses whether that was a stale (delayed) quote or a transformed (modified) one **the log cannot say** — fact 1. The 10-02 miss can: the quote was 3.2 s old at placement and the indicative ask had already moved +0.04 past the limit by the post-placement re-read (a new stamp) and +0.08 by cancel — stale *and* moving, on a feed whose prices are off-grid (modified). Both mechanisms are real; PR-2 must handle both: a fresh quote at placement (staleness), a buffer plus the parity floor (modification), and early re-pricing (either).
5. **Fills happen inside ~55 s or not at all.** 42 fills ≤ 53 s (38 inside 1 s); sixteen 120-s waits produced nothing. Waiting longer is not the lever; being marketable at placement is — and if not, re-pricing early is (DD-4) — **on both legs** (rev 5: the STC ladder escalates too; the uncovered window is the cost that rev 2's "the STC ladder is not re-priced" ignored: nine rung-1 misses each left the shares uncovered for 121 s before the floor rung filled in under a quarter-second).

**Sim gap (FC-116 D1):** the replay fills a base-mode BTC at `min(limit, ask_c)` against a cent-quantised modeled book, so it can never miss — `roll_skips` post-placement terminals read 0 on lake chains by construction. The sim is not wrong about the rule; live is wrong about the quote.

## Goals

- **PR-1 (S, no behaviour change):** every roll leg's placement, timeout/cancel and fill carries the quote it was priced from (bid/ask/mid/spread/sizes/broker stamp/age/feed), a same-second second read, the IEX stock quote and intrinsic at placement, the tick legality of the limit, the fresh quote at cancel time, the broker's fill price and fill latency — on the existing events *and* on one new per-leg terminal event (`call_roll_leg_settled`) that FC-113 and any later study can read as a table on both services (DD-9 gives the CC service a sink).
- **PR-2 (M, rev 5):** base-mode limits are **marketable and tick-legal by construction, priced from a quote read immediately before the order** — BTC at `max(ask + buffer, parity + 0.01)` snapped *up*; STC at `bid − buffer` snapped *down*, never below the invariant floor — and a leg that has not filled inside its window is **re-priced at a pre-committed escalation**: the BTC up to twice inside its 120 s total (3 × 40 s), the STC primary up to `stc_escalation_rungs` (2) times at `−(n+1)·buffer` in **30 s windows** before the floor rung, so a rung-1 miss leaves the shares uncovered for ~45 s instead of 135 s. The credit invariant is tested on the buffered, snapped limits at every site, so a filled roll still cannot net a debit. FC-113 (a) derived per-position budget (now including PR-1's diagnostic seconds) and (b) monotonic poll accounting land in the same PR.
- Both profiles, identical defaults (Symmetry; the profiles differ only in `itm_trigger_ratio`).
- The sim keeps driving the real roller: the rule change flows into the replay without a second fill model.

## Design decisions (with rejected alternatives)

### DD-1 — Two PRs, instrumentation first (done); the data gate is WAIVED — PR-2's defaults are fixed by pre-committed rules on the 50 placements in hand and re-read, not re-gated, after the first live week

**Sequencing (rev 5):** PR-1 is merged (`f4f12fa`) and live. PR-2 builds now. The operator signed Q2 ("open PR-2 immediately") on 2026-09-26 and has since waived every wait-and-see gate for this FC, so rev 2's "≥ 3 trading days of PR-1 data" readout and the "PR-2 merges behind the gate decision" clause are **withdrawn**. What replaces them is a set of rules the plan commits to now, each evaluated on the data in §Context, each re-evaluable by anyone from the same fields later, and each movable by a yaml edit (DD-7), never a rebuild:

- **Rule B (buffer):** `marketable_buffer_per_share` is the smallest nickel-grid value covering **≥ 90 %** of the observed one-sided offsets — BTC `limit − fill` on fills, STC rung-1 `fill − limit` on fills, and rung-1 miss offsets (`prior_rung_miss_offset`, the only measurement a miss leaves). On the 42 offsets of §Context: 0.05 covers 33/42 (79 %), **0.10 covers 40/42 (95 %)**, 0.15 and 0.20 cover the same 40/42, 0.35 covers 42/42. **0.10 stands**, both profiles. The two uncovered: +0.11 in the seller's favour (no action needed) and the 0.35 CC miss, which the STC escalation rungs (Rule E) now own. *Cost of 0.15 instead:* +$10/contract of eligibility on every roll for no additional coverage on this data. *Cost of 0.05:* 5 of the 9 rung-1 misses would still miss.
- **Rule W (windows):** a leg's first window is the smallest multiple of the 5 s poll that exceeds the longest observed time-to-fill of a *marketable* order on that leg by ≥ 25 %. STC: max 23.0 s → **30 s** (`stc_rung_timeout_seconds`). BTC: the four resting fills were 5.1 / 15.7 / 24.4 / 52.9 s; the first three fit **40 s**, and the 52.9 s one — a limit AT the true ask — would be re-priced at +0.20 and fill at the NBBO (fact 3: the fill price is the NBBO, not the limit). `btc_fill_timeout_seconds` stays 120 total, split 3 × 40 s (DD-4). *Cost of a 30 s STC window vs 120:* nothing observed — no STC fill ever landed between 23 s and 120 s; it saves ~90 s of uncovered time per rung-1 miss (9 of 21 placements). *Cost of 60 s instead of 30:* +30 s uncovered per miss and +180 s on the per-position worst case for no observed fill gained.
- **Rule E (escalation):** `stc_escalation_rungs = 2` at step = buffer (`−0.20`, `−0.30` from the rung-1 basis), then the floor. With 0.10 / 2 rungs: 8 of the 9 observed misses fill on the first escalation rung at ~45 s, and the 0.35 miss reaches the floor (as today) after 3 × 45 s plus ≤ 9 s of basis reads — the same ~135–144 s today's single rung costs, so the tail is no worse and every other miss is 90 s better. *Cost of 3 rungs:* +45 s worst case, catches the 0.35 case on rung 4 (`−0.40`) instead of the floor — same fill price (NBBO), so no benefit on the data. *Cost of 0 rungs:* today's 135 s uncovered window on every miss.
- **Readout after the first live week (informational, not a gate):** the same three distributions from `call_roll_leg_settled` on both services (`fill_vs_limit` on rung-1 / attempt-0 fills; `prior_rung_miss_offset` with `prior_rung_disposition`; `fill_latency_s`), plus the sampler's `ask_minus_intrinsic` and re-read pairs. ### DD-3 — PR-2 pricing rule (rev 5): buffer + parity floor + tick snap in base mode, priced from a quote read immediately before each order; imminence keeps its pad but is tick-snapped too

**Helper (T7 single signature):** `src/strategy/limit_pricing.py::snap_limit(price: Decimal, tick: Decimal, direction: Literal["up", "down"]) -> Decimal` — ceiling or floor to the tick grid; pure arithmetic, no tick lookup, no warning. The roller composes it: `tick = tick_size(price, underlying)` (tick from the **unsnapped** value, so the $3.00 boundary is decided once; `_warn_if_unverified` fires there), `float(snap_limit(price, tick, "up"))`. It is *not* `round_to_tick` (which rounds sells **up** for the entry leg's never-above-the-ask bound); both stay, each docstring names the other. **S2:** every operand is `_dec(x)` (`limit_pricing._dec`, `Decimal(str(float(x)))`); the buffer is `Decimal(str(buffer))`; no `Decimal(float)` anywhere in the roller.

**Base mode (R1, R3):**

```
buffer   = Decimal(str(config.rolling_marketable_buffer_per_share))     # default 0.10
parity   = _dec(stock_bid) - _dec(strike)                                # IEX stock bid, real-time
btc_raw  = max(_dec(ask) + buffer, parity + Decimal("0.01"))             # deep-ITM floor: marketable regardless of feed
btc      = snap_limit(btc_raw, tick_size(btc_raw, underlying), "up")
stc_raw  = _dec(basis_bid) - buffer                                      # None if <= 0; basis_bid per the rung rule below
stc      = snap_limit(stc_raw, tick_size(stc_raw, underlying), "down")
```

The parity term (R3) is feed-independent: a call's true ask cannot sit below intrinsic at the stock bid, so `parity + 0.01` is marketable whenever the indicative ask is quoted *below parity* — the modified-quote failure mode a buffer cannot bound. It has not bound on any observed leg (PR-1's `limit_minus_intrinsic` was 3.58 and 4.07 on the two BTC placements; the sampler's `ask_minus_intrinsic` positive on 5/5); it is kept because it costs nothing when it does not bind and is the only floor that survives a wrong feed. On failure of the stock read the parity term is omitted for that placement, never a raise.

**Which quote prices which order (new in rev 5 — the quote-age fact):** PR-1 measured the BTC quote at **3.2 s / 4.1 s** old at placement and the STC rung-1 quote at **6.0 s** (the pre-BTC read, aged by the BTC's own fill wait), and every post-placement re-read carried a new stamp that had already moved 0.03–0.21. The evaluation-time quote is therefore a *screening* quote only. In `_execute_roll`, the pre-BTC block (today: one `get_option_quote(new_symbol)` and the STC-side re-check) becomes `_price_legs_at_execution(opportunity)`: `get_option_quote(old_symbol)` + `get_stock_quote(underlying)` (try/except → parity omitted) + `get_option_quote(new_symbol)`, **three main-thread pricing reads before any order exists** (no exposure; FC-089's missing socket timeout is the same risk the existing `fresh` read carries today). The BTC limit is re-derived from the fresh ask by the formula above (`btc_quote_source="execution"`; if the fresh read is unusable, the evaluation-time quote is used and logged `"evaluation"`), the STC rung-1 limit from the fresh new-symbol bid, and the invariant is re-run on the pair → `credit_gone_at_execution` (unchanged reason; the skip event gains `btc_limit_fresh` and `evaluated_btc_limit`). `opportunity['btc_limit']`, `opportunity['stc_limit']`, `fallback_candidates[0]['stc_limit']` and `stc_quote_rung1` are replaced exactly as the S9 sites do today. Expected effect: BTC `quote_age_s` at placement ≤ ~1 s.

**Imminence mode:** `btc = snap_up(max(mid + 0.05, parity + 0.01))`, `stc = snap_down(mid − 0.05)` — the pad is unchanged, the buffer does **not** stack (imminence already crosses half the spread; FC-078 DD-2), the parity floor **does** apply to the BTC (it is a marketability floor, not a pad). The mode is decided once, at evaluation (DD-5).

**The STC primary ladder (rev 5 — replaces "rung 1 and rung 2"):** in `_rungs`, `floor = snap_up(_dec(btc_filled_price) + min_credit)` (rounds **up** — down would breach the invariant). Rungs on the primary candidate, in order:

- **Rung 1 (primary):** `max(snap_down(basis₁ − buffer), floor)` with `basis₁ = min(pre_btc_bid, fresh_bid)` — `fresh_bid` from one bounded pricing read (`_bounded_read(get_option_quote, new_symbol, timeout=_PRICING_READ_TIMEOUT_SECONDS)`, a new module constant **3.0 s**) taken right after the BTC fill, so the basis is at most the BTC's time-to-fill stale and never *higher* than the pre-BTC bid. On timeout/empty the basis is the pre-BTC bid alone (`stc_quote_source="pre_btc"`). *Why `min` and not the fresh bid alone:* the indicative bid's error is symmetric (±0.03–0.11 on fills, +0.02–0.35 on misses) and the 10-05 leg saw it swing 7.43 → 7.22 → 7.51 around a true bid of 7.40; the lower of two recent reads is the safer estimate of the true bid, and a lower limit costs nothing on the fill (fact 3: fills land at the NBBO bid).
- **Escalation rungs 2 … 1+E** (`E = config.rolling_stc_escalation_rungs`, default **2**): rung `1+n` = `max(snap_down(basisₙ₊₁ − (n+1)·buffer), floor)` with `basisₙ₊₁ = min(basisₙ, fresh_bidₙ)` from one bounded pricing read taken immediately after rung `n`'s zero-fill settle (fallback: `basisₙ`, source `"prior_basis"`). A rung whose computed limit is not strictly above the floor **is** the floor rung — the ladder yields the floor there and stops escalating (so E is a maximum, never a count of placements).
- **Floor rung:** `floor`, yielded only when strictly below the last rung placed (today's `0 < floor_price < primary_limit` guard, applied to the last escalation limit). The sell-at-cost floor remains: on paper 9/9 floor placements filled within 0.21 s at 18–59 % through the fill price; on a live exchange it may be rejected by limit-order price protection — a **real-money gate row** (see §Risks, "the floor rung on a live exchange"), not a design change, because under this ladder the floor is reached only after the buffered primary and E escalations missed — 1 of the 9 observed misses would have reached it.
- **Fallback rungs** (`fallback_strike_attempts`, today's rungs 3+): other candidates from the stored legal list, each re-quoted (today's main-thread read, now bounded at 3 s), priced by the base/imminence rule **with the buffer**, re-validated through `validate_roll`, then the invariant against the actual BTC fill — unchanged in structure.

The PR-1 `rung` field keeps its meaning — the ladder position as placed, 1-based (primary 1, escalations 2…1+E, floor next, fallbacks after). New log-only fields disambiguate: `rung_kind ∈ {primary, escalation, floor, fallback}`, `escalation_index` (0 on the primary, n on escalation n, null otherwise), `basis_bid`, `stc_quote_source ∈ {fresh, pre_btc, prior_basis, chain}`, `primary_limit` (rung 1's limit, on every rung of the same ladder), and on every rung after the first `prior_rung_limit`, `prior_rung_disposition` and `prior_rung_miss_offset` (`prior_rung_limit − fill`, null unless the prior rung's disposition was `timeout_canceled` / `terminal_no_fill` and this rung filled at the broker's price) plus `primary_miss_offset = primary_limit − fill` (the DD-1 Rule B measurement, regardless of how many rungs lie between). `quote_reused_from_rung` on the floor names the rung whose quote it carries (the last escalation, or 1 when E = 0).

**The invariant is tested on the buffered, snapped limits at every site:** the candidate screen (`_legal_candidates`, with the buffered `btc_limit` and each candidate's buffered `stc_limit`), the execute-time re-price (`_price_legs_at_execution`), each DD-4 BTC re-price, every STC rung (the `max(…, floor)` on the primary ladder; the explicit test against the actual BTC fill on fallbacks). Consequence: a candidate that netted less than `2·buffer + up to 2 ticks` (**up to $28/contract on a nickel-tick name at 0.10; $20 on a penny grid**) is no longer legal. **Buffer economics (T4, rev 5 numbers):** the buffer is an *eligibility* bound, not a cost — 40 of 42 fills landed strictly through the limit, at the NBBO, so a roll that fills nets the same credit it would have at the raw quote. None of the 21 completed rolls ($55–$380) would have been ineligible at 0.10 (the tightest, NVDA 09-08, placed 5.47 / 5.95 → buffered 5.60 / 5.85 → +$25 ≥ the $0 floor).

*Why not the deleted percentage pads (FC-078 DD-1)?* They were applied *after* the invariant was screened on raw quotes, so a pad could push a screened-legal pair into a debit. Here the buffer is applied *before* every invariant test; it can only make a marginal roll ineligible. Flat dollars anchored to the tick, not a percentage of a modified quote.

*Rejected — 0.05 default (rev 1):* covers 33/42 observed offsets (79 %); 0.10 covers 40/42 (95 %), and one of the two it misses was favourable. *Rejected — 0.15:* same 40/42 coverage, +$10/contract of eligibility. *Rejected — spread-scaled `max(0.10, k·spread)`:* the indicative spread is itself a modified number (fact 3: the 10-05 STC quote was 7.43/7.45 — a 2-cent spread on a nickel-grid contract); add the term if the readout shows misses scale with the quoted spread. *Rejected — a longer timeout:* fact 5. *Rejected — pricing rung 1 from the fresh bid alone:* a fresh indicative bid is no closer to the truth than the pre-BTC one (10-05: 7.22 at placement, 7.51 at cancel, true 7.40); `min` of the two is. *Rejected — reusing the pre-BTC quote for rung 1 (rev 2):* its age at placement is the BTC's time-to-fill (0.04 s–120 s). *Rejected — a different endpoint:* `get_option_latest_quote` *is* the latest quote; the feed, not the endpoint, is the cause. *Deferred — expiry-day rolls before 15:30:* Q4.

### DD-4 — PR-2 re-pricing on BOTH legs (rev 5): the BTC up to twice inside its 120 s total; the STC primary escalates in 30 s windows before the floor; every re-price follows a terminal zero-fill settle; `rejected` is terminal

**BTC (unchanged from rev 2 except where marked):** `btc_fill_timeout_seconds` (120) is the BTC leg's **total** poll budget, split evenly across `btc_reprice_attempts + 1` windows (default **2 → 40 s / 40 s / 40 s**). *Rev 5 check against the corrected latencies:* 17/21 BTC fills landed inside 1 s and three of the four resting fills inside 25 s; the 52.9 s fill (08-26, AT its limit) is the one a 40 s window would have re-priced — at `+0.20` it fills at the NBBO ask (fact 3), so the cost is one extra placement and ~13 s, not price. Attempt `n` (n = 0, 1, 2) is priced at **`ask_fresh + (n+1)·buffer`** (then the parity floor and the snap, DD-3) — a committed ladder. Attempt 0's `ask_fresh` is the execute-time read (DD-3, `_price_legs_at_execution`). Sequence in `_execute_roll`, extracted as `_place_and_settle_btc(opportunity) -> Tuple[Optional[Dict], Dict]`:

1. Place attempt `n`; `_poll_order_fill(order_id, timeout=window)` (`window = timeout // (attempts + 1)`).
2. Terminal fill (full or partial) → today's path, unchanged.
3. `status == "rejected"` → **terminal `btc_rejected`** (both the synchronous refusal and the after-placement branch of `_execute_roll`), never re-priced (R2): a rejection is the broker's verdict on the order, not on the price.
4. Poll returns `None` → `_cancel_and_settle` (15 s). Settle `None` → `_unknown_disposition`, **no second order, ever**. Settle with `filled_qty > 0` → the ladder (a cancel that lost the race is a fill). Settle terminal zero-fill and `n < attempts` → step 5. Terminal zero-fill from the poll itself (`expired` / `canceled` — the replay's path, `BacktestAlpacaClient._order_expired`) → step 5 without a cancel.
5. **Re-price:** fresh `get_option_quote(old_symbol)` and `get_stock_quote(underlying)` — **bounded pricing reads** (`_bounded_read(..., timeout=_PRICING_READ_TIMEOUT_SECONDS)`); on a timed-out option read the attempt is priced from the prior attempt's ask basis (`btc_quote_source="prior_basis"`), on a timed-out stock read the parity term is omitted; recompute the limit by DD-3 at `(n+2)·buffer` (**same pricing mode as the evaluation**); fresh `get_option_quote(new_symbol)` → fresh STC rung-1 limit by DD-3, which **replaces `opportunity['stc_limit']`, `fallback_candidates[0]['stc_limit']` and `stc_quote_rung1`** (S9); re-run the invariant. Invariant fails → terminal skip `credit_gone_at_reprice` (a new `skip_reason`, sibling of `credit_gone_at_execution`; nothing is live). Exhausted → `call_roll_btc_timeout_canceled` as today, with `attempts` on it. **The fresh option read of step 5 is also attempt `n`'s `cancel_quote_*` set** (one read, two uses — ruling A's "after the settle" ordering holds; the deferred `_post_settle_quote` for a re-priced attempt reads only the IEX stock quote).

**Time budget (S4, recomputed with PR-1's diagnostic seconds — confirmation-pass item 6):** PR-2 replaces `_PER_POSITION_BUDGET_SECONDS = 600` (`wheel_engine.py`) with `per_position_budget_seconds(config)` in `wheel_engine.py`, computed once in `run_rolling_cycle`, used by the `cycle_budget_exhausted` guard and logged on `roll_cycle_started` as `per_position_budget_seconds`:

```

reread   = _DIAG_READ_TIMEOUT_SECONDS                                       # 2.0 — one bounded re-read per placement (the floor reuses the prior rung's)
```

## Risks & mitigations

- **Risk:** the buffer makes marginal rolls ineligible (`2·buffer + ≤ 2 ticks`, up to $28/contract on nickel names). **Mitigation:** it costs nothing on fills (NBBO, 40/42); none of the 21 completed rolls would have been excluded; DD-1 Rule B can lower it by yaml; `0.00` is legal; the invariant guarantees no debit.
- **Risk:** the re-price (BTC) or escalation (STC) places a second live order after a cancel whose settle lied. **Mitigation:** DD-4 — only after a *terminal zero-fill* settle; `None` → unknown-disposition and the ladder stops; `rejected` → terminal (BTC) / next rung with the miss offset nulled (STC); T-10(c)/(h), T-17(f)/(g).
- **Risk — the uncovered window:** the STC ladder is where the shares are uncovered; a design that lengthens it is worse than today even if it fills more often. **Mitigation:** 30 s rungs (Rule W: no STC fill observed between 23 s and 120 s in 30 placements); the typical miss resolves at ~45 s instead of ~135 s; the worst case on the primary ladder (every escalation missing) is ~140 s against today's 135 s — one of nine observed misses would have walked it; `stc_escalation_rungs: 0` restores today's shape by yaml.
- **Risk — the floor rung on a live exchange (real-money gate, unchanged in substance):** `btc_fill + min_credit` sat 18–59 % through the fill price on the nine paper floor fills; Cboe / NOM limit-order price protection can reject (or re-price) a sell limit that far through the NBBO, so live the floor may come back `stc_rejected` → fallback rungs → `call_roll_naked_exposure`. Rev 5 does not change the floor (it is the invariant's guarantee and the only feed-independent rung) but makes it the *last* rung on the primary ladder, after escalations that sit 0.10–0.30 through — well inside any published protection band on a $3+ contract (not verified here; the gate row carries the verification on the first live roll). Exact gate row (replaces the PR-1 "Rung-2 exchange price protection" row in `docs/CLAUDE.md`): `| The floor rung (`btc_fill + min_credit`, 18–59 % through the fill price on paper) may be rejected by exchange limit-order price protection on a live account; it is reached only after the buffered primary and `stc_escalation_rungs` escalations (0.10–0.30 through) missed — 1 of 9 observed misses. A live rejection is `stc_rejected` → fallback rungs → `call_roll_naked_exposure`; the shares are then uncovered until the next `/run`. Verify on the first live roll that reaches the floor; if rejected, cap the floor at `fresh_bid − (E+1)·buffer` (a yaml-free code change under this FC's rollback) | both | FC-120 (verify on the first live floor placement; do not assume the floor rung exists live) | open |`.
- **Risk:** the derived per-position budget (496 s) is lower than today's literal (600) because the ladder is faster, so a cycle now *admits* a third worst-case position. **Mitigation:** 3 × 496 = 1488 ≤ 1500; the seam arithmetic is `1500 + 60 ≤ 1800` by construction; `roll_cycle_budget_misconfigured` refuses a yaml that breaks it; the function and the seam test derive the same number independently (T-9).
- **Risk:** identity churn — `src/` PRs re-replay every stored sim key. **Mitigation:** DD-6 (i); PR-1 already moved it; the battery's pins prove the re-replay; `ENGINE_VERSION` makes the boundary queryable.
- **Risk — a bounded pricing read on the uncovered window:** the fresh basis read for rung 1 / an escalation rung sits between a BTC fill (or a settle) and the next STO placement. **Mitigation:** `_PRICING_READ_TIMEOUT_SECONDS = 3.0` with a stated fallback (DD-3; T-17(c), T-19); the alternative — pricing from the pre-BTC quote — was measured 6 s stale on 10-05 with the bid 0.21 away, and would be up to 120 s stale after a slow BTC.
- **Risk:** `snap_limit` composition on an unverified root warns once per process and prices at nickels above $3 by assumption. **Mitigation:** `tick_class_unverified` is alert-visible; add the root to `VERIFIED_PENNY_PROGRAM_ROOTS` when onboarding. **Periodic `ppind` re-verification (T-LOW):** penny-program membership changes; a root that leaves it prices at nickels below $3 and is *rejected* live, not routed. Quarterly operator check, recorded as a real-money gate row (R6).
- **Risk:** the post-settle quote and the second read are new network calls near the cycle deadline. **Mitigation:** try/except, `None` on failure, after the disposition is final; the per-position guard reserves the full worst case.
- **Risk — live tick handling (T2, corrected):** Alpaca live routes a non-conforming limit to NOM and **rejects only if it cannot**; the roller's `btc_rejected` / `stc_rejected` paths exist but have never fired on paper. PR-2 makes the roller's limits tick-legal, which **removes the roller from FC-088's gate row — it does not by itself clear a gate**: live rejected-path handling and exchange price protection are separate rows (R6). Rev 1's "closes a real-money precondition" was an over-claim.
- *(Rev 2's "rung-2 price protection is a live divergence" bullet is superseded by "the floor rung on a live exchange" above: nine paper floor fills now, 18–59 % through, and the floor is the last primary-ladder rung under rev 5.)*
- **Risk:** the Mock fixtures make every roll test fail at once if a new config attribute is missed. **Mitigation:** PR-2 item 9 lists the exact attributes (four new ones in rev 5); `Mock() // 2` raising is the tripwire, not a surprise.
