## Context

The covered-call roller (FC-078, live on the CC service since 2026-09-17 via FC-100) prices its buy-to-close at `round(ask, 2)` (`evaluate_roll_opportunity`) and its sell-to-open at `round(bid, 2)` (`_stc_limit_from_quote`) from `AlpacaClient.get_option_quote`, polls 120 s (`_poll_order_fill`), then cancels (`_cancel_and_settle`). **Five facts the plan rests on (rev 5 wording; fact 1 is now history, fact 5 is corrected):**

1. **Until PR-1 the log carried no quote.** Pre-10-02 `call_roll_btc_placed` / `call_roll_stc_placed` logged `limit_price`, `contracts`, `pricing_mode` (BTC only) — not bid, ask, stamp, feed or sizes; `get_option_quote` returned all of them and the roller dropped them. Quote age at placement is unknowable for every pre-PR-1 event above. Since `f4f12fa` every leg row carries the DD-2 set (`_placement_fields`, `_reread_fields`, `_post_settle_quote`, `_log_leg_settled`).
2. **The feed is Alpaca's `indicative` feed** on both services (`alpaca_client.OPTION_QUOTE_FEED`, passed explicitly by `get_option_quote`; the OPRA agreement is unsigned — FC-072 §Rollout 0, `docs/CLAUDE.md` §Trading APIs / §Real-money gates). Alpaca's description: *"trades are delayed and quotes are modified."* The endpoint is `get_option_latest_quote` — the latest quote, not a snapshot. The stock side is IEX, real-time (`AlpacaClient.get_stock_quote`, with its own `timestamp`).
3. **Off-grid limits are the fingerprint of a modified quote, not the miss mechanism.** 15 of 29 BTC limits and 12 of 21 rung-1 limits were off the tick grid; an NBBO price cannot be, so those quotes were not NBBO prices. But on-grid misses exist (BTC: AAPL 1.39 08-27, IWM 0.68 09-02; rung 1: five of the nine misses) and off-grid limits filled 10/15 — the grid does not decide the fill. **Every one of the 42 fills was on the grid; 40 were strictly through their limit and two (08-26 AAPL 1.60, 09-28 NVDA 2.01) filled at it after resting 53 s and 24 s**: the placed limit is the binding price only when it sits exactly at the true far side; otherwise fills happen at the NBBO. Live consequence of an off-grid limit: Alpaca *"will attempt to route the order to the NOM. If they are not able to route the order to NOM, the order will be rejected"* (support article "Options Pricing Increments and Options Order Handling") — the same rule `limit_pricing.round_to_tick`'s docstring and FC-088 state. Paper accepted all of them; FC-072 added `round_to_tick` for the entry legs; the roller never calls it. PR-1 now logs `limit_on_tick` on every leg: false on all four post-PR-1 placements above $3.
4. **Alpaca paper fills against the real NBBO** (paper-trading docs: matched against the NBBO; a buy limit fills only when `limit ≥ best ask`). So a BTC at the indicative ask fills only when the true ask is at or below it; the 21 fills say the indicative ask sat 0.00–0.07 *above* the true ask; the seven real-market misses say it sat *below* for 120 s. For the six pre-PR-1 misses whether that was a stale (delayed) quote or a transformed (modified) one **the log cannot say** — fact 1. The 10-02 miss can: the quote was 3.2 s old at placement and the indicative ask had already moved +0.04 past the limit by the post-placement re-read (a new stamp) and +0.08 by cancel — stale *and* moving, on a feed whose prices are off-grid (modified). Both mechanisms are real; PR-2 must handle both: a fresh quote at placement (staleness), a buffer plus the parity floor (modification), and early re-pricing (either).
5. **Fills happen inside ~55 s or not at all.** 42 fills ≤ 53 s (38 inside 1 s); sixteen 120-s waits produced nothing. Waiting longer is not the lever; being marketable at placement is — and if not, re-pricing early is (DD-4) — **on both legs** (rev 6: the STC primary rung runs a 30 s window and then the floor — the uncovered window is the cost that rev 2's "the STC ladder is not re-priced" ignored: nine rung-1 misses each left the shares uncovered for 121 s before the floor rung filled in under a quarter-second; escalation rungs in front of the floor are a knob shipped off, because the floor fills at once — R6-A).

**Sim gap (FC-116 D1):** the replay fills a base-mode BTC at `min(limit, ask_c)` against a cent-quantised modeled book, so it can never miss — `roll_skips` post-placement terminals read 0 on lake chains by construction. The sim is not wrong about the rule; live is wrong about the quote.

## Goals

- **PR-1 (S, no behaviour change):** every roll leg's placement, timeout/cancel and fill carries the quote it was priced from (bid/ask/mid/spread/sizes/broker stamp/age/feed), a same-second second read, the IEX stock quote and intrinsic at placement, the tick legality of the limit, the fresh quote at cancel time, the broker's fill price and fill latency — on the existing events *and* on one new per-leg terminal event (`call_roll_leg_settled`) that FC-113 and any later study can read as a table on both services (DD-9 gives the CC service a sink).
- **PR-2 (M, rev 6):** base-mode limits are **marketable and tick-legal by construction, priced from a bounded quote read immediately before the order** — BTC at `max(ask + buffer, parity + 0.01)` snapped *up*; STC at `bid − buffer` snapped *down*, never below the invariant floor — and a leg that has not filled inside its window is **re-priced only after the roller's own cancel-and-settle returns a terminal zero-fill**: the BTC up to twice inside its 120 s total (3 × 40 s); the STC primary rung runs a **30 s window** and then the floor rung (`snap_up(btc_fill + min_credit)`), so a rung-1 miss leaves the shares uncovered for ~31 s instead of 121 s. `stc_escalation_rungs` ships as a knob at **0** on both profiles (bounds [0, 3]) — the escalation rungs' only function is a hedge against live exchange price protection (Q7), a real-money gate with its own knob (R6-O). The credit invariant is tested on the buffered, snapped limits at every site, so a filled roll still cannot net a debit; `min_net_credit_per_contract` rises to **$0.20/contract** (one tick after `snap_up`; covers the round-trip fee). FC-113 (a) — a per-position budget derived by the leaf module `src/strategy/roll_budget.py` and **measured** by a test — and (b) monotonic poll accounting with a two-read settle floor land in the same PR, with the quiet data-plane read path on its own circuit breaker.
- Both profiles, identical defaults (Symmetry; the profiles differ only in `itm_trigger_ratio`).
- The sim keeps driving the real roller: the rule change flows into the replay without a second fill model.

## Non-goals

PR-2's rule is feed-agnostic and remains correct on OPRA (one tick through the NBBO is the ordinary marketable limit); it is the paper-era mitigation, not a substitute. Q1 blocks the DD-1 gate *decision*, not the opening of either PR.
- Not the STC ladder's terminals (naked-exposure / partial), cancel-then-settle, `_TERMINAL_ORDER_STATUSES`, or the one-live-order invariant. *(Rev 5 narrowed this non-goal and rev 6 narrows it back: the per-rung window shrinks to 30 s because the data showed the uncovered window is the cost; escalation rungs CAN be inserted between the primary and the floor by yaml (`stc_escalation_rungs`) but ship at 0, so the shipped ladder shape — primary, floor, fallbacks — is today's. The floor rung, the fallback rungs and every terminal keep their semantics; DD-4 / DD-5.)*

## Design decisions (with rejected alternatives)

### DD-1 — Two PRs, instrumentation first (done); the data gate is WAIVED — PR-2's defaults are fixed by pre-committed rules on the 50 placements in hand and re-read, not re-gated, after the first live week

**Sequencing (rev 5):** PR-1 is merged (`f4f12fa`) and live. PR-2 builds now. The operator signed Q2 ("open PR-2 immediately") on 2026-09-26 and has since waived every wait-and-see gate for this FC, so rev 2's "≥ 3 trading days of PR-1 data" readout and the "PR-2 merges behind the gate decision" clause are **withdrawn**. What replaces them is a set of rules the plan commits to now, each evaluated on the data in §Context, each re-evaluable by anyone from the same fields later, and each movable by a yaml edit (DD-7), never a rebuild:

- **Rule B (buffer; restated rev 6 — R6-K):** `marketable_buffer_per_share` is the smallest nickel-grid value covering **≥ 90 %** of the observed **unfavourable-direction** offsets — the observations that say how far a limit sat from the true market when it did NOT fill: STC rung-1 `primary_miss_offset` (rung-1 limit − the floor fill; 9 observations) and, on BTC misses, `cancel_quote_ask − limit` where PR-1 measured it (10-02: +0.08). **The BTC misses are censored:** the six pre-PR-1 BTC timeouts carry no offset at all (fact 1) — they say "> 0" and nothing more — so the miss population under-represents the BTC side, and the readout adds every post-PR-1 BTC miss as it arrives. Fills in the favourable direction (BTC `limit − fill`, rung-1 `fill − limit`; 33 of the 42 offsets in §Context) do **not** size the buffer — a limit already through the true far side fills at any buffer ≥ 0 — they stay in §Context as the eligibility and economics evidence (DD-3). *Reconciled with rev-4 ruling E in writing:* ruling E excluded floor fills from the FILL population because a floor fill is not a rung-1 fill; here a floor fill is used only as the measurement of the true bid **~121 s after** rung 1 was placed — which is why a miss offset is a **lower bound** on the buffer that would have filled rung 1 at placement, not an estimate of it (the true bid moved during the window; on 10-05 the indicative bid swung 7.43 → 7.22 → 7.51 around a true bid of 7.40). On the 9 miss offsets (0.02–0.35): **8/9 ≤ 0.10 — 6/9 strictly inside 0.10, 2 at exactly 0.10 (a limit AT the true bid, which fills when the market ticks — the 23–53 s shape), one at 0.35**; 0.05 covers 4/9; the one measured BTC miss (0.08) is inside 0.10. **0.10 stands**, both profiles. Under rev 6 a rung-1 miss costs ~31 s (R6-Q), not 121 s, so a buffer one tick too small costs one 30 s window and a buffer too large costs eligibility (`2·buffer + ≤ 2 ticks`, DD-3). *Cost of 0.15:* +$10/contract of eligibility; covers the same 8/9. *Cost of 0.05:* 5 of the 9 misses still miss (+31 s each).
- **Rule W (windows; a taste choice, stated as such — R6-R / PO-9):** a leg's first window is the smallest multiple of the 5 s poll that exceeds the longest observed time-to-fill of a *marketable* order on that leg by ≥ 25 %; any other margin is defensible from the same data and the readout re-evaluates it. STC: max 23.0 s → **30 s** (`stc_rung_timeout_seconds`). ### DD-3 — PR-2 pricing rule (rev 6): buffer + parity floor + tick snap in base mode, priced from a bounded quote read immediately before each order; imminence keeps its pad on each leg's FIRST placement and is tick-snapped; every re-price is base-mode

**Helper (T7 single signature):** `src/strategy/limit_pricing.py::snap_limit(price: Decimal, tick: Decimal, direction: Literal["up", "down"]) -> Decimal` — ceiling or floor to the tick grid; pure arithmetic, no tick lookup, no warning. The roller composes it: `tick = tick_size(price, underlying)` (tick from the **unsnapped** value, so the $3.00 boundary is decided once; `_warn_if_unverified` fires there), `float(snap_limit(price, tick, "up"))`. It is *not* `round_to_tick` (which rounds sells **up** for the entry leg's never-above-the-ask bound); both stay, each docstring names the other. **S2:** every operand is `_dec(x)` (`limit_pricing._dec`, `Decimal(str(float(x)))`); the buffer is `Decimal(str(buffer))`; no `Decimal(float)` anywhere in the roller.

**Base mode (R1, R3):**

```
buffer   = Decimal(str(config.rolling_marketable_buffer_per_share))     # default 0.10
parity   = _dec(stock_bid) - _dec(strike)                                # IEX stock bid, real-time
btc_raw  = max(_dec(ask) + buffer, parity + Decimal("0.01"))             # deep-ITM floor: marketable regardless of feed
btc      = snap_limit(btc_raw, tick_size(btc_raw, underlying), "up")
stc_raw  = _dec(basis_bid) - buffer                                      # None if <= 0; basis_bid per the rung rule below
stc      = snap_limit(stc_raw, tick_size(stc_raw, underlying), "down")
```

The parity term (R3) is feed-independent: a call's true ask cannot sit below intrinsic at the stock bid, so `parity + 0.01` is marketable whenever the indicative ask is quoted *below parity* — the modified-quote failure mode a buffer cannot bound. It has not bound on any observed leg (PR-1's `limit_minus_intrinsic` was 3.58 and 4.07 on the two BTC placements; the sampler's `ask_minus_intrinsic` positive on 5/5); it is kept because it costs nothing when it does not bind and is the only floor that survives a wrong feed. On failure of the stock read the parity term is omitted for that placement, never a raise. Log-only on every BTC row (R6-R): `stock_quote_source ∈ {execution, reprice, evaluation, none}` (which IEX read supplied the parity term; `none` = omitted) and `parity_floor_applied` (bool — the parity term, not `ask + buffer`, was the binding operand), so a parity-bound limit is countable in the readout and in the DD-6 before/after split.

**Which quote prices which order (new in rev 5 — the quote-age fact):** PR-1 measured the BTC quote at **3.2 s / 4.1 s** old at placement and the STC rung-1 quote at **6.0 s** (the pre-BTC read, aged by the BTC's own fill wait), and every post-placement re-read carried a new stamp that had already moved 0.03–0.21. The evaluation-time quote is therefore a *screening* quote only. In `_execute_roll`, the pre-BTC block (today: one `get_option_quote(new_symbol)` and the STC-side re-check) becomes `_price_legs_at_execution(opportunity)`: `get_option_quote(old_symbol)` + `get_stock_quote(underlying)` (failure → parity omitted) + `get_option_quote(new_symbol)`, **three bounded pricing reads before any order exists** — each through `_bounded_read(..., timeout=_PRICING_READ_TIMEOUT_SECONDS)` on the quiet data-plane path (rev 6, R6-G / R6-J, S-LOW-6 / T-LOW-2: rev 5 left these three on the main thread with no bound, which the budget could not count; now each costs ≤ 3.0 s and the budget counts all three). The BTC limit is re-derived from the fresh ask by the formula above (`btc_quote_source="execution"`; if the fresh read is unusable, the evaluation-time quote is used and logged `"evaluation"`; **if both are unusable — `ask ≤ 0` on each — the position is a true skip, `call_roll_skipped` with `skip_reason="quote_unusable"`, no order placed — R6-E**), the STC rung-1 limit from the fresh new-symbol bid, and the invariant is re-run on the pair → `credit_gone_at_execution` (unchanged reason; the skip event gains `btc_limit_fresh` and `evaluated_btc_limit`). `opportunity['btc_limit']`, `opportunity['stc_limit']`, `fallback_candidates[0]['stc_limit']` and `stc_quote_rung1` are replaced exactly as the S9 sites do today. Expected effect: BTC `quote_age_s` at placement ≤ ~1 s.

**Imminence mode (R6-C):** on each leg's **first** placement (BTC attempt 0; STC rung 1) `btc = snap_up(max(mid + 0.05, parity + 0.01))`, `stc = max(snap_down(mid − 0.05), floor)` — the pad is unchanged, the buffer does **not** stack (imminence already crosses half the spread; FC-078 DD-2), the parity floor **does** apply to the BTC (it is a marketability floor, not a pad). The mode is decided once, at evaluation (DD-5), and `pricing_mode` keeps naming it on every row of the position. **After ANY zero-fill settle, every re-price uses the base-mode formula from the fresh quote** — BTC attempt n ≥ 1 at `snap_up(max(ask_fresh + (n+1)·buffer, parity + 0.01))`, an escalation rung (E > 0) at `max(snap_down(basis − (n+1)·buffer), floor)` — because a pad that rested a full 40 s / 30 s window has proven insufficient and a second pad would rest again; the attempt / rung index n is the same in both modes (there is no separate imminence ladder). Every placed row logs `limit_formula ∈ {base, imminence}` so the readout can split the two (T-10 / T-17 carry imminence cases).

**The STC primary ladder (rev 6 — rung 1, then the floor; escalation rungs only when `stc_escalation_rungs > 0`, shipped 0 — R6-A):** in `_rungs`, `floor = snap_up(_dec(btc_filled_price) + min_credit)` (rounds **up** — down would breach the invariant; with `min_net_credit_per_contract = 0.20` → `min_credit = 0.002`/share, so the floor is the BTC fill plus one tick — R6-L). Rungs on the primary candidate, in order:

- **Rung 1 (primary):** `max(snap_down(basis₁ − buffer), floor)` with `basis₁ = min(pre_btc_bid, fresh_bid)` — `fresh_bid` from one bounded pricing read (`_bounded_read(get_option_quote, new_symbol, timeout=_PRICING_READ_TIMEOUT_SECONDS)`, **3.0 s**) taken right after the BTC fill, so the basis is at most the BTC's time-to-fill stale and never *higher* than the pre-BTC bid. **`pre_btc_bid` is the LAST pre-placement read of the new symbol (R6-D):** `_price_legs_at_execution`'s read when the BTC filled on attempt 0, or the re-price's `get_option_quote(new_symbol)` read when it filled on attempt n ≥ 1 (at most one BTC window — 40 s — old at the fill, plus the settle if the fill landed inside it); never the original execute-time read after a re-price. On timeout/empty the basis is `pre_btc_bid` alone (`stc_quote_source="pre_btc"`). *Why `min` and not the fresh bid alone:* the indicative bid's error is symmetric (±0.03–0.11 on fills, +0.02–0.35 on misses) and the 10-05 leg saw it swing 7.43 → 7.22 → 7.51 around a true bid of 7.40; the lower of two recent reads is the safer estimate of the true bid, and a lower limit costs nothing on the fill (fact 3: fills land at the NBBO bid). **When `snap_down(basis₁ − buffer) ≤ floor` the rung placed IS the floor** (R6-R): logged with `rung=1`, `rung_kind="floor"`, `quote_reused_from_rung=null` (it carries the fresh read it was priced from), and no second floor rung follows (T-8).
- **Escalation rungs 2 … 1+E** (`E = config.rolling_stc_escalation_rungs`, **shipped 0** on both profiles, bounds [0, 3] — "off until Q7 is read on a live account"): rung `1+n` = `max(snap_down(basisₙ₊₁ − (n+1)·buffer), floor)` with `basisₙ₊₁ = min(basisₙ, fresh_bidₙ)` from one bounded pricing read taken immediately after rung `n`'s zero-fill settle (fallback: `basisₙ`, source `"prior_basis"`) — the **base-mode** formula in both pricing modes (R6-C). An escalation rung is placed **only after the roller's own `_cancel_and_settle` returned a terminal zero-fill** (R6-B); after a terminal zero-fill from the primary poll (`canceled` by another actor, `expired`) the ladder skips the escalation rungs and goes to the floor (`call_roll_stc_unfilled` → the next non-escalation rung, as today). After a **synchronous rejection** of a rung (`_place_stc` returns `None`, nothing to settle) the next *priced* rung takes a fresh bounded basis read (R6-F); the floor stays quote-free. A rung whose computed limit is not strictly above the floor **is** the floor rung — the ladder yields the floor there and stops escalating (E is a maximum, never a count of placements).
- **Floor rung:** `floor`, yielded only when strictly below the **last limit the primary ladder computed — placed or synchronously rejected** (today's `0 < floor_price < primary_limit` guard, applied to the last primary-ladder limit; R6-R: a rejected rung's limit was still the ladder's position, and a floor at or above it would be the same price tried again — if that rung was rejected for price, the floor is further through and will be rejected too, which is exactly the Q7 signature the gate row names). Attempt 0's `ask_fresh` is the execute-time read (DD-3, `_price_legs_at_execution`). Sequence in `_execute_roll`, extracted as **`_place_and_settle_btc(opportunity) -> Tuple[Optional[Dict[str, Any]], Dict[str, Any]]`** = `(filled_order, leg)` (R6-R — defined, not implied): `filled_order` is the terminal order dict with `filled_qty > 0` (full or partial) or `None`; when `None`, `leg` is the result dict `_execute_roll` returns **verbatim** (`{'success': False, 'reason': 'btc_rejected' | 'btc_timeout_canceled' | 'btc_disposition_unknown', …}` — the terminal has already been emitted, exactly one); when not `None`, `leg` is the attempt context the STC ladder and the fill events need: `attempt`, `btc_limit`, `btc_order_id`, `btc_fields` (the placement set), `pre_btc_bid` and `stc_limit` (R6-D: from the LAST pre-placement read of the new symbol), `btc_quote_source`, `stock_quote_source`, `limit_formula`, `polls`, `settle_polls`, `elapsed`, `t0`:

1. Place attempt `n`; `_poll_order_fill(order_id, timeout=window)` (`window = timeout // (attempts + 1)`).
2. Terminal fill (full or partial) → today's path, unchanged.
3. `status == "rejected"` → **terminal `btc_rejected`** (both the synchronous refusal and the after-placement branch of `_execute_roll`), never re-priced (R2): a rejection is the broker's verdict on the order, not on the price.
4. Poll returns `None` → `_cancel_and_settle` (15 s window, **≥ 2 reads** — R6-I). Settle `None` → `_unknown_disposition`, **no second order, ever**. Settle with `filled_qty > 0` → the ladder (a cancel that lost the race is a fill). Settle terminal zero-fill and `n < attempts` → step 5. **A terminal zero-fill from the PRIMARY poll — `canceled` by another actor, or `expired` at the 16:00 DAY expiry — stays terminal exactly as today (R6-B):** `call_roll_btc_timeout_canceled` with `disposition="terminal_no_fill"`, `attempts = n + 1`, no re-price. A re-price is only ever placed over an order the roller itself canceled and saw settle: that is the only case in which it knows the book it re-prices against is the one the order sat in. (The replay's `_order_expired` answers `expired` from the primary poll, so **the replay never re-prices** — DD-6 restates this as a contract.)
5. **Re-price:** fresh `get_option_quote(old_symbol)` and `get_stock_quote(underlying)` — **bounded pricing reads** (`_bounded_read(..., timeout=_PRICING_READ_TIMEOUT_SECONDS)`, on the quiet data-plane path — R6-J); on a timed-out option read the attempt is priced from the prior attempt's ask basis (`btc_quote_source="prior_basis"`), on a timed-out stock read the parity term is omitted (`stock_quote_source="none"`); recompute the limit by the DD-3 **base-mode** formula at `(n+2)·buffer` whatever the evaluation's `pricing_mode` (R6-C — a pad that rested a full window has proven insufficient; `limit_formula="base"` on the row); fresh `get_option_quote(new_symbol)` → fresh STC rung-1 limit by DD-3, which **replaces `opportunity['stc_limit']`, `fallback_candidates[0]['stc_limit']` and `stc_quote_rung1`** (S9) and **becomes rung 1's `pre_btc_bid`** (R6-D — the last pre-placement read, at most one BTC window old at the fill, plus the settle if the fill landed inside it; never `_price_legs_at_execution`'s original read); re-run the invariant. **Could not re-price → the leg's terminal is `call_roll_btc_timeout_canceled` with `reprice_skipped_reason ∈ {credit_gone, quote_unusable}`** (R6-E): `credit_gone` when the invariant fails on the fresh pair (the row carries `btc_limit_fresh` and `stc_limit_fresh`), `quote_unusable` when `ask ≤ 0` on both the fresh read and the prior basis. It is **never a `call_roll_skipped`** — that event's contract is "no order was ever placed" (`call_roller.py` module docstring) and a BTC was placed and canceled — so `_ALREADY_TALLIED_ROLL_FAILURES` is unchanged and the replay counts it under `btc_timeout_canceled` (`_POST_PLACEMENT_ROLL_FAILURES`, `simulator.py`). Otherwise emit `call_roll_btc_repriced` (`attempt`, `prior_limit`, `new_limit`, `prior_order_id`, both quote sets, `stock_bid`, `limit_formula`) and place; back to step 1 with `n + 1`. Exhausted → `call_roll_btc_timeout_canceled` as today, with `attempts` on it. **The fresh option read of step 5 is also attempt `n`'s `cancel_quote_*` set** (one read, two uses — ruling A's "after the settle" ordering holds; the deferred `_post_settle_quote` for a re-priced attempt reads only the IEX stock quote). At **execute time** (before any order) the symmetric case is a true skip: both BTC quotes unusable (fresh and evaluation) → `call_roll_skipped` with `skip_reason="quote_unusable"` (R6-E; DD-3).
6. Every placement emits its own `call_roll_leg_settled` row (`attempt = n`) — a leg that was re-priced twice produces **three** rows.

**STC (rev 6 — rung 1, then the floor; escalation rungs are a knob shipped at 0):** the ladder of DD-3 runs in `_attempt_stc` with **`_poll_order_fill(order_id, timeout=config.rolling_stc_rung_timeout_seconds)`** (default **30 s**) on every rung — primary, escalations (when `stc_escalation_rungs > 0`), floor and fallbacks — instead of the BTC's 120 s. Every rung transition is cancel-then-settle exactly as today (`_settle_live_rung` → `_cancel_and_settle`, ≥ 2 reads — R6-I); a rung that settles with a fill is that rung's success; a settle that returns `None` stops the ladder (`stc_disposition_unknown`); a terminal zero-fill lets the next rung place. **With E = 0 (shipped) the rung after the primary is the floor, which is quote-free: between rung 1's zero-fill settle and the floor's placement there is no pricing read at all**, so a rung-1 miss is covered ≈ 31–32 s after placement (R6-Q: a 30 s window is 7 reads at the 5 s poll — reads at 0, 5, …, 30 s; the cancel settles in one read on paper; the floor filled in ≤ 0.21 s on 9/9). With E > 0, exactly one bounded basis read (3.0 s at worst; it doubles as the prior rung's `cancel_quote_*`) sits between a zero-fill settle and the next escalation rung; the floor and fallback rungs need no basis read (fallbacks keep their own re-quote). **An escalation rung is placed only after the roller's own `_cancel_and_settle` returned a terminal zero-fill (R6-B); a terminal zero-fill from the primary poll (`canceled` by another actor, `expired`) emits `call_roll_stc_unfilled` and goes to the next non-escalation rung — the floor — exactly as today.** A synchronous rejection (`_place_stc` returns `None`, nothing to settle) → the next *priced* rung (escalation or fallback) takes its own fresh bounded basis read (R6-F); the floor stays quote-free. Why the data forces the 30 s window: the nine rung-1 misses each cost **121 s of uncovered shares** before a floor that filled in < 0.21 s; no STC fill was ever observed between 23 s and 120 s. **Timing with defaults (R6-Q):** typical miss ≈ 31–32 s uncovered; per-rung worst on the real clock ≈ 49–52 s (30 s window + the trailing read + a ~15 s settle + the bounded reads; the budget's *modeled* worst in the table above is larger by design); **the pre-floor worst case is one rung** (today: one 135 s rung; rev 5 proposed up to three 45 s rungs ≈ 144 s). *Rejected:* keeping 120 s rungs (every miss sits 121 s before a floor that fills at once). *Rejected:* escalating by default (R6-A: 9/9 floor fills landed at the NBBO bid in ≤ 0.2 s, so an intermediate rung buys no price on paper and adds two placements/cancels and up to ~100 s of uncovered time on the 0.35-class tail; its only function is a hedge against live price protection — Q7's own knob, R6-O). *Rejected:* escalating after a primary-poll `canceled` / `expired` (R6-B: the roller did not cancel that order and cannot know what book it is re-pricing against). *Rejected:* escalating without the floor (the floor is the invariant's guarantee and the only rung that is marketable regardless of feed).

```
R      = ORDER_READ_WORST_SECONDS                       # 9.0  one get_order_by_id at the retry worst
C      = ORDER_ACTION_WORST_SECONDS                     # 2.0  one place_option_order / one cancel_order
G      = max(POLL_INTERVAL_SECONDS, R)                  # 9    the trailing read of a window: the monotonic loop reads, THEN checks the deadline; sleep(min(5, remaining)) never overshoots
P, D   = PRICING_READ_TIMEOUT_SECONDS, DIAG_READ_TIMEOUT_SECONDS          # 3.0, 2.0
SETTLE = C + max(CANCEL_SETTLE_MIN_READS * R, CANCEL_SETTLE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS + R)   # 2 + max(18, 24) = 26  (R6-I: two reads regardless of RTT; the 15 s window is a floor)
A      = btc_reprice_attempts + 1;   Wb = btc_fill_timeout_seconds // A;   Ws = stc_rung_timeout_seconds
btc_attempt = 3P + C + D + (Wb + G) + SETTLE            # 9 + 2 + 2 + 49 + 26 = 88 — 3P: old option, stock, new option (execute-time on attempt 0, re-price after; all bounded — S-LOW-6 / T-LOW-2)
btc    = A * btc_attempt + (A - 1) * D                  # + the re-priced attempts' deferred cancel-time stock read; the last attempt's fill lands inside its settle
stc_rung = basis + C + reread + (Ws + G) + SETTLE + D + post_option
           basis: P on the primary (fresh bid) and on each fallback (its re-quote); 0 on an escalation (its basis IS the prior rung's post_option) and on the floor (quote-free)
           reread: D on every rung but the floor (it reuses rung 1's quote);  post_option: P when an escalation follows this rung, else D;  the trailing D is the cancel-time stock read
stc    = Σ over primary, E escalations, the floor, F fallbacks
budget = ceil(btc + stc)
```

### DD-6 — The sim mirror (rev 6): no adapter change; base-mode fills identical except where the live parity term binds; the replay NEVER re-prices or escalates — a contract (R6-B); `ENGINE_VERSION` moves in every byte-pinned copy and in the two era sets (R6-M)

## Risks & mitigations

- **Risk:** the buffer makes marginal rolls ineligible (`2·buffer + ≤ 2 ticks`, up to $28/contract on nickel names). **Mitigation:** it costs nothing on fills (NBBO, 40/42); none of the 21 completed rolls would have been excluded; DD-1 Rule B can lower it by yaml; `0.00` is legal; the invariant guarantees no debit.
- **Risk:** the re-price (BTC) or escalation (STC) places a second live order after a cancel whose settle lied. **Mitigation:** DD-4 — only after a *terminal zero-fill* settle; `None` → unknown-disposition and the ladder stops; `rejected` → terminal (BTC) / next rung with the miss offset nulled (STC); T-10(c)/(h), T-17(f)/(g).
- **Risk — the uncovered window:** the STC ladder is where the shares are uncovered; a design that lengthens it is worse than today even if it fills more often. **Mitigation (rev 6):** a 30 s rung-1 window (Rule W: no STC fill observed between 23 s and 120 s in 30 placements) and then the floor, which filled 9/9 in ≤ 0.21 s — the typical miss is covered ≈ 31–32 s after placement instead of 121 s and the pre-floor worst case is ONE rung (R6-A / R6-Q); no escalation rung ships, so nothing is inserted in front of the floor; `stc_escalation_rungs > 0` lengthens the window by yaml only, deliberately, as Q7's hedge.
- **Risk — exchange price protection on a live account (real-money gate; R6-O):** `btc_fill + min_credit` sat 18–59 % through the fill price on the nine paper floor fills; Cboe / NOM limit-order price protection can reject (or re-price) a sell limit that far through the NBBO — and on a sub-$1 contract, whose band is absolute-dollar and narrower, even a buffered rung may be far enough through. Live, any rejected rung is `stc_rejected` → the next rung → fallbacks → `call_roll_naked_exposure`. Rev 6 does not change the floor (it is the invariant's guarantee and the only feed-independent rung) and does not pre-build the response — the pre-committed response is recorded in the gate row (PR-2 item 13, verbatim): re-place once at `fresh_bid − buffer`, then a %-of-fresh-bid cap knob set from the exchange band, both as a code change under this FC's rollback once the band has been read (Q7). The signature to watch for in the runbook: `call_roll_naked_exposure` after `stc_rejected` rows.
- **Risk:** the derived per-position budget (567 s) is **lower** than today's literal (600) while the modeled ladder is *longer* than the real one — a cycle admits a position with 567 s left that today needed 600. **Mitigation:** the budget is measured, not asserted (T-19: the worst-case ladder on both profiles ≤ the function); two worst-case positions = 1134 ≤ 1500 and a third is deferred, not squeezed; the seam arithmetic `933 + 567 + 60 ≤ 1800` leaves 240 s; `Config` refuses a yaml that breaks the bound at load and `roll_cycle_budget_misconfigured` pages if code and config disagree at runtime (R6-H); the number does not shrink against rev 5's 496 because R6-G / R6-I count the trailing read, the cancel, the three execute-time reads and a settle floor that rev 5 did not — stated in DD-4 so nobody mistakes 567 for an optimisation.
- **Risk — the data-plane breaker (R6-J):** pricing reads now share a breaker with diagnostic reads; a quote-endpoint outage can open it (5 failures / 60 s) and then every pricing read returns `None` for up to 60 s. **Mitigation:** every pricing read has a stated fallback (DD-3: the evaluation quote, the prior basis, the floor), so a leg still places or skips loudly; the order-plane breaker that gates `get_order_by_id` / `place_option_order` / `cancel_order` is untouched by it, which is the property that matters — a diagnostic storm can no longer block the settle (T-20).
- **Risk — `min_net_credit_per_contract` 0.20 now (R6-L):** eligibility tightens by $0.20/contract. **Mitigation:** it refuses nothing observed (the tightest completed roll netted +$25 buffered); the floor rung becomes the BTC fill plus one tick, which is what makes "credit-only" true after fees on a live account.
- **Risk:** identity churn — `src/` PRs re-replay every stored sim key. **Mitigation:** DD-6 (i); PR-1 already moved it; the battery's pins prove the re-replay; `ENGINE_VERSION` makes the boundary queryable.
- **Risk — FC-121 PR-2 (#137) is in review in parallel** and may merge before or after this PR. It deletes `src/backtesting/screen.py` and `src/backtesting/reporting/bq_writer.py` (so `ENGINE_VERSION` has two byte-pinned copies, not three), moves `engine_identity` (one more cold battery Saturday), and relocates `config_hash` to `src/backtesting/reporting/config_hash.py` and `DEFAULT_LOOKBACK_DAYS` to `src/backtesting/scenarios/identity.py`. **Mitigation:** FC-120 PR-2 touches none of those import sites; it bumps `ENGINE_VERSION` in every copy present on main at build time (DD-6 (ii), PR-2 item 8) and whichever of the two PRs merges second rebases and re-runs the pinning tests before merge.
- **Risk — a bounded pricing read on the uncovered window:** the fresh basis read for rung 1 / an escalation rung sits between a BTC fill (or a settle) and the next STO placement. **Mitigation:** `_PRICING_READ_TIMEOUT_SECONDS = 3.0` with a stated fallback (DD-3; T-17(c), T-19); the alternative — pricing from the pre-BTC quote — was measured 6 s stale on 10-05 with the bid 0.21 away, and would be up to 120 s stale after a slow BTC.
- **Risk:** `snap_limit` composition on an unverified root warns once per process and prices at nickels above $3 by assumption. **Mitigation:** `tick_class_unverified` is alert-visible; add the root to `VERIFIED_PENNY_PROGRAM_ROOTS` when onboarding. **Periodic `ppind` re-verification (T-LOW):** penny-program membership changes; a root that leaves it prices at nickels below $3 and is *rejected* live, not routed. Quarterly operator check, recorded as a real-money gate row (R6).

- **Risk — live tick handling (T2, corrected):** Alpaca live routes a non-conforming limit to NOM and **rejects only if it cannot**; the roller's `btc_rejected` / `stc_rejected` paths exist but have never fired on paper. - **Risk:** the Mock fixtures make every roll test fail at once if a new config attribute is missed. **Mitigation:** PR-2 item 10 lists the exact attributes (four new ones); `Mock() // 2` raising is the tripwire, not a surprise.
