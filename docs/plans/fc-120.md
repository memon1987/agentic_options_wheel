## Context

The covered-call roller (FC-078, live on the CC service since 2026-09-17 via FC-100) prices its buy-to-close at `round(ask, 2)` and its sell-to-open at `round(bid, 2)` from `AlpacaClient.get_option_quote`, polls 120 s, then cancels. In its first week on the CC service the first leg filled **1 of 4** times. This plan first establishes, from the logs and the fills, what can and cannot be said about why — and then specifies the instrumentation that makes the next miss diagnosable (PR-1) and the pricing rule that should stop most of them (PR-2).

**Five facts the plan rests on:**

1. **The log carries no quote.** `call_roll_btc_placed` / `call_roll_stc_placed` log `limit_price`, `contracts`, `pricing_mode` (BTC only) — not the bid, ask, quote timestamp, feed or sizes. `get_option_quote` already returns all five (`alpaca_client.py:409-421`, the broker's `timestamp`); the roller drops them. Quote age at placement is therefore **unknowable for every event above**. That gap is PR-1's deliverable.
2. **The feed is Alpaca's `indicative` feed**, on both services (`OPTION_QUOTE_FEED`, `alpaca_client.py:47`; the OPRA agreement is unsigned — FC-072 §Rollout 0). Alpaca's own description of `indicative`: *"trades are delayed and quotes are modified"* (options latest-quotes reference, `feed` parameter). The endpoint is `OptionLatestQuoteRequest` → `get_option_latest_quote` — the **latest quote, not a snapshot**; the FC entry's "snapshot" is a misnomer. The stock side is IEX, real-time.
3. **Every placed limit is off the tick grid; every fill is on it.** GOOGL/NVDA/AAPL options at or above $3.00 trade in $0.05 increments (penny program, `limit_pricing.tick_size`). Placed: 3.38, 6.94, 9.03, 11.71, 9.46, 5.47, 8.07, 4.07. Filled: 6.90, 8.95, 5.45, 8.05, 4.00, 2.65, 1.60. A "modified" quote that lands between ticks cannot be an NBBO price, and a limit placed at it is at best one tick inside the real book on the wrong side. Alpaca **paper** accepted the off-tick limits; FC-072 added `round_to_tick` for the entry legs precisely because a real account rejects them (`limit_pricing.py:426`). The roller never calls it.
4. **Alpaca paper fills against the real NBBO** ("orders … matched against the best available current market price (NBBO)"; a buy limit fills only when `limit ≥ best ask`; paper-trading docs). So a BTC at the indicative ask fills only when the true ask is at or below it. The wheel's 0.02–0.07 improvements say the indicative ask sat *above* the true ask those times; GOOGL's three misses say it sat *below* — for 120 s, on a day (09-22) when the stock was falling into the close (C350 10/02 was bought back by the profit-taker at 4.70 the next morning), which is the direction a merely *delayed* ask would have over-priced, not under-priced. That points at modification more than delay, but **the log cannot prove it either way** — fact 1.
5. **Fills happen at once or not at all.** Ten fills, all at the first or third 5 s poll; three 120 s waits produced nothing. Waiting longer is not the lever; being marketable at placement is.

**Sim gap (FC-116 D1):** the replay fills a base-mode BTC at `min(limit, ask_c)` against a cent-quantised modeled book, so it can never miss — `roll_skips` post-placement terminals read 0 on lake chains by construction. The sim is not wrong about the rule; live is wrong about the quote.

## Goals

- **PR-1 (S, no behaviour change):** every roll leg's placement, timeout/cancel and fill carries the quote it was priced from (bid/ask/mid/spread/sizes/broker timestamp/age/feed), the tick legality of the limit, the fresh quote at cancel time, the broker's fill price and fill latency — on the existing events *and* on one new per-leg terminal event (`roll_leg_settled`) that FC-113 and any later study can read as a table.
- **PR-2 (S–M):** base-mode limits are **marketable and tick-legal by construction** — BTC at the ask plus a bounded buffer, snapped *up* to the legal tick; STC at the bid minus the same buffer, snapped *down* — and a BTC that has not filled at half the timeout is **re-priced once from a fresh quote**. The credit invariant `STC_limit − BTC_limit ≥ min_net_credit` is still tested on the placed limits at all four sites, so a filled roll still cannot net a debit.
- Both profiles, identical defaults (Symmetry; the profiles differ only in `itm_trigger_ratio`).
- The sim keeps driving the real roller: the rule change flows into the replay without a second fill model.

## Non-goals

- **Not the OPRA feed.** Signing the OPRA agreement / Algo Trader Plus is an operator purchase and already a real-money precondition (FC-072 §Rollout 0). PR-2's rule is feed-agnostic and remains correct on OPRA (one tick through the NBBO is the ordinary marketable limit); it is the paper-era mitigation, not a substitute. See Open questions Q1.
- Not the STC ladder's structure, rung count, naked-exposure terminals, cancel-then-settle, `_TERMINAL_ORDER_STATUSES`, or the one-live-order invariant.
- Not the schedule (15:30 ET; FC-078 DD-4) — Q4.
- Not FC-113's admission-time deadline or monotonic poll accounting; PR-2 only re-derives the per-position constant its own change moves (DD-5).
- Not a new BigQuery table; the events reach BQ through the existing log sink as every `call_roll_*` event does.
- Not the imminence-mode pad value ($0.05) or threshold.

## Design decisions (with rejected alternatives)

### DD-1 — Two PRs, instrumentation first; PR-2's defaults are confirmed against PR-1's data, not guessed

PR-1 ships alone and runs for **≥ 5 trading days or ≥ 4 placements across the two services, whichever comes later**, before PR-2 merges. The one number PR-2 needs from it is the sign and size of `fill_vs_limit` and of `(cancel_quote_ask − quote_ask)` on the misses: if the indicative ask reliably sits within one tick of the true ask, the $0.05 buffer + tick snap in DD-3 is enough; if it sits $0.10–0.20 inside, `marketable_buffer_per_share` ships at 0.10. The rule and the keys do not change either way; only the default might. *Rejected:* shipping both in one PR — the defaults would be fixed on four GOOGL events and ten wheel fills, and a wrong buffer either wastes credit on every roll or leaves the miss rate where it is.

### DD-2 — PR-1 field set (additive; no event renamed, no field removed)

Every field below is derived from data the roller already holds or one extra `get_option_quote` at cancel time. Names reuse FC-072's vocabulary (`quote_*`, `quote_age_s`, `quote_feed`) so the readout can be joined with the entry legs' `*_sale_executing` rows.

**On `call_roll_btc_placed` and `call_roll_stc_placed`:** `quote_bid`, `quote_ask`, `quote_mid`, `quote_spread`, `quote_bid_size`, `quote_ask_size`, `quote_ts` (broker stamp, ISO-8601, `None` if absent), `quote_age_s` (`limit_pricing.quote_age_seconds(ts)`, `None` if no stamp), `quote_feed`, `limit_on_tick` (bool: `Decimal(limit) % tick_size(limit, underlying) == 0`), `limit_vs_quote` (BTC: `limit − ask`; STC: `bid − limit`; ≥ 0 means at-or-through the quoted side), `leg` (`"btc"`/`"stc"`), `rung` (STC only; 1-based), `pricing_mode` (STC gains it — today only the BTC event has it), `placed_at` (ISO, `clock.now_utc()`). The BTC quote is the one `evaluate_roll_opportunity` priced from (carry `btc_quote` on the opportunity dict — it is not there today; only `btc_limit` is); the STC rung-1 quote is the pre-BTC re-check's `fresh` dict; rungs ≥ 3 use the per-rung `quote`; rung 2 has no quote of its own (it is the invariant floor) — its `quote_*` are the rung-1 values with `quote_reused_from_rung: 1`.

**On `call_roll_btc_timeout_canceled` and on every STC rung that ends zero-fill (the `call_roll_stc_unfilled` error event, and a new `call_roll_stc_timeout_canceled` trade event emitted from `_settle_live_rung` when the settle shows zero fill — today that path logs nothing for the rung itself):** all placement fields above, plus a **fresh quote fetched after the settle** as `cancel_quote_bid`, `cancel_quote_ask`, `cancel_quote_ts`, `cancel_quote_age_s`, `quote_drift` (`cancel_quote_ask − quote_ask` for a BTC; `quote_bid − cancel_quote_bid` for an STC — positive means the market moved away), `leg_elapsed_s` (monotonic, `time.monotonic()` from just before `place_option_order` to the settle result), `order_submitted_at` (Alpaca's, from `get_order_by_id`), `order_status`. The extra quote is one HTTP call on a path that has just spent 135 s; on failure the fields are `None` and a `quote_refresh_failed`-style breadcrumb is logged — never a raise.

**On `call_roll_btc_filled` / `call_roll_stc_filled`:** `limit_price` (the BTC one lacks it today), `filled_avg_price` (already `filled_price`; keep the name, add nothing), `fill_vs_limit` (BTC: `limit − fill`; STC: `fill − limit`; ≥ 0 by construction of a limit order), `fill_latency_s` (Alpaca `filled_at − submitted_at`, both already returned by `get_order_by_id`; `None` if either missing), `leg_elapsed_s`, `polls` (count of `get_order_by_id` reads), plus the placement `quote_*` set.

**New event `roll_leg_settled`** (`log_trade_event`, `strategy="roll_call"`): exactly **one per placed leg**, emitted at the leg's terminal disposition alongside the existing event, with `leg`, `rung`, `order_id`, `disposition ∈ {filled, partial, timeout_canceled, terminal_no_fill, rejected, unknown}`, `limit_price`, `filled_avg_price`, `filled_qty`, `requested_qty`, the placement `quote_*` set, the `cancel_quote_*` set (`None` on fills), `fill_vs_limit`, `quote_drift`, `fill_latency_s`, `leg_elapsed_s`, `polls`, `pricing_mode`, `symbol`, `underlying`, `request_id` (already injected by the logger). It is the row FC-113 wants (`leg_elapsed_s` is the wall-clock the FC-113 problem statement says nobody measures) and the row a fill-rate study reads with one `WHERE event_type="roll_leg_settled"`. *Rejected:* widening `roll_cycle_completed` with per-leg arrays (that event is a `log_system_event` message, not a `jsonPayload.event_type` row — FC-100 DD-6's triage note); a new BQ table (a log-sink row is already a table). *Rejected:* replacing the existing events with the new one — the alert policies and `test_roll_fills.py` pin the existing names.

**Not logged:** the option's last trade. `OptionLatestTradeRequest` accepts `feed=indicative`, but Alpaca says indicative *trades are delayed*, so a "marketable vs last print" field would compare a modified quote to a delayed print. Q5 leaves the door open; PR-1 does not depend on it.

**No behaviour change, verified structurally:** the only new I/O is the post-settle quote on the timeout path (after the disposition is final). The dry-run branch (`call_roll_dry_run`) gains the BTC `quote_*` set and `limit_on_tick` so the PR-2 dry-run can be read from it.

### DD-3 — PR-2 pricing rule: buffer + tick snap in base mode; imminence keeps its pad but is tick-snapped too

New helper `src/strategy/limit_pricing.py::snap_limit(value: Decimal, underlying: str, side: str) -> Tuple[float, bool]` — `side="buy"` rounds **up** to the legal tick (ceiling; more aggressive for a buyer), `side="sell"` rounds **down** (floor; more aggressive for a seller); returns `(price, snapped)`. It reuses `tick_size` and `_warn_if_unverified`; it is *not* `round_to_tick`, which rounds sells up and exists for the entry leg's "never above the ask" bound. Both helpers stay; a docstring on each names the other.

Base mode (`call_roller.py:352-353` and `_stc_limit_from_quote`, `:526-544`):

```
buffer      = config.rolling_marketable_buffer_per_share          # default 0.05
btc_limit   = snap_limit(Decimal(ask) + buffer, underlying, "buy")   # ≥ ask + buffer, on tick
stc_limit   = snap_limit(Decimal(bid) − buffer, underlying, "sell")  # ≤ bid − buffer, on tick; None if ≤ 0
```

Imminence mode: `btc = snap_limit(mid + 0.05, "buy")`, `stc = snap_limit(mid − 0.05, "sell")` — the pad is unchanged and the buffer does **not** stack (imminence already crosses half the spread; adding the buffer would pay the spread twice, the exact double-count FC-078 DD-2 avoided).

Rung 2's floor (`_rungs`, `:969`): `floor = snap_limit(btc_filled_price + min_credit, "sell")` would round *down* below the invariant — so the floor rounds **up** (`snap_limit(..., "buy")` semantics; expose `direction` explicitly rather than overloading `side`): the rung stays ≥ the invariant minimum. Rungs ≥ 3: base/imminence rule as above, then the invariant test against the actual BTC fill, unchanged.

The invariant is tested on the **buffered, snapped** limits at every existing site (candidate screen `_legal_candidates`, pre-BTC re-check, order construction, every rung). Consequence, stated plainly: a candidate that netted less than `2·buffer + up to 2 ticks` (≈ $10–18/contract on a nickel-tick name) is no longer legal. Those rolls were credit-illusory — their limits sat inside the true book — and on the wheel the typical roll credit is $200+ (09-18: $205; 09-17 evaluated: $456).

*Why not the deleted `btc_limit_over_ask_pct` / `stc_limit_under_bid_pct` (FC-078 DD-1 "pads break/waste the credit invariant")?* Those were **percentage** pads applied *after* the invariant was screened on the raw quotes, so a pad could push a screened-legal pair into a debit. Here the buffer is applied **before** every invariant test, so it cannot break the invariant — it can only make a marginal roll ineligible. And it is a flat dollar amount anchored to the tick, not a percentage of a modified quote.

*Rejected — spread-scaled buffer `max(0.05, k·spread)`:* the spread on the indicative feed is itself a modified number (fact 3), so `k·spread` scales the buffer by an artefact; a $11.71 GOOGL ask with a $0.20 quoted spread would get $0.05 either way at `k = 0.25`. If PR-1 shows the misses scale with the quoted spread, add the term then (one knob, additive).

*Rejected — a longer timeout (c):* fact 5. Ten fills inside 15 s; three 120 s waits fetched nothing. Doubling the wait doubles the FC-113 exposure for no expected fills.

*Rejected — a different endpoint (d):* `get_option_latest_quote` *is* the latest quote. `OptionSnapshotRequest` returns the same latest quote plus greeks. The feed, not the endpoint, is the cause — and the feed is an entitlement (Non-goals, Q1).

*Deferred — expiry-day rolls before 15:30 (e):* the one expiry-day cycle (09-18) is the one that filled; the misses were T-1 and T-10/T-9. Q4.

### DD-4 — PR-2 BTC re-price: once, at half the timeout, from a fresh quote, with the invariant re-tested; the STC ladder is not re-priced

`btc_fill_timeout_seconds` (120) becomes the BTC leg's **total** poll budget, split evenly across `btc_reprice_attempts + 1` placements (default 1 → 60 s + 60 s). Sequence in `execute_roll`:

1. Place the BTC at the DD-3 limit; `_poll_order_fill(order_id, timeout=120 // 2)`.
2. Terminal fill (full or partial) → today's path, unchanged.
3. Not terminal (`None`) → `_cancel_and_settle` (unchanged, 15 s). Settle `None` → `_unknown_disposition` (unchanged — a re-price is never attempted over an order whose disposition is unknown). Settle with `filled_qty > 0` → proceed into the ladder (a cancel that lost the race is a fill, unchanged). Settle with zero fill, `attempts_left > 0` → step 4. Terminal zero-fill from the poll itself (`expired`/`canceled` — the sim's path) → step 4 without a cancel.
4. **Re-price:** fresh `get_option_quote(old_symbol)`; recompute the limit by DD-3 (same pricing mode as the evaluation — do not re-decide imminence mid-execution); fresh `get_option_quote(new_symbol)` and re-run the pre-BTC invariant check against it. Invariant fails → terminal skip `credit_gone_at_reprice` (a new `skip_reason`, sibling of `credit_gone_at_execution`; nothing is live, so it is a skip, not a failure). Quote unusable (`ask ≤ 0`) → `call_roll_btc_timeout_canceled` with `reprice_skipped_reason="btc_quote_unavailable"`. Otherwise emit `call_roll_btc_repriced` (`attempt`, `prior_limit`, `new_limit`, `prior_order_id`, both quote sets) and place; `_poll_order_fill(timeout=60)`; back to step 2 with `attempts_left − 1`. Exhausted → `call_roll_btc_timeout_canceled` as today, with `attempts` on it.
5. Every placement (first and re-priced) goes through the same `roll_leg_settled` emission (DD-2) — a re-priced leg produces **two** rows, one per order.

The STC ladder is *not* re-priced: it already has a retry structure (rung 2 at the floor, rungs 3+ on other candidates), each rung's quote is fetched when reached, and rung 2 at `btc_fill + min_credit` is far through the book by construction (09-18: 6.90 against an 8.95 bid, filled in the same second). *Rejected:* re-pricing rung 1 — it would add 75 s to the naked window for a rung the floor already covers.

**Time budget.** Per-leg worst case: BTC `2 × (60 + 15) = 150 s` (was 135 s); STC `4 × (120 + 15) = 540 s` (unchanged). Per-position worst = **690 s** (was 675 s; FC-113 (a) says the shipped 600 s constant was already wrong). PR-2 replaces `_PER_POSITION_BUDGET_SECONDS = 600` with the derived expression `_btc_leg_worst_case(config) + _stc_ladder_worst_case(config)` computed in `wheel_engine.run_rolling_cycle` from the leg constants and the config (timeout, `fallback_strike_attempts`, reprice attempts), asserted by a test (T-9), and logged on `roll_cycle_started`. `_CYCLE_BUDGET_SECONDS = 1500` is untouched; two ITM positions still fit (1380 s). FC-113 (b) monotonic polling and (c) the admission-time deadline stay FC-113's; the plan's `## Amendments` slot records whichever lands first so the two do not both claim the constant. *Rejected:* keeping 600 and adding the re-price anyway — that widens the exact seam FC-113 was filed about.

### DD-5 — What must NOT change (the contract PR-2's reviewer checks first)

- The terminal-event contract: exactly one terminal per evaluated position (`call_roller.py:18-27`); `credit_gone_at_reprice` is a `call_roll_skipped` reason, added to `simulator._ALREADY_TALLIED_ROLL_FAILURES` beside `credit_gone_at_execution` (`test_roll_fills.py` `TestTheAllowlistIsTheRollersOwnVocabulary` will fail otherwise).
- At most one STO live at any instant; cancel-then-settle on every rung transition; `_TERMINAL_ORDER_STATUSES`; `_CANCEL_SETTLE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS`; `stc_failed_naked_exposure` / partial terminals; rung count (`test_the_ladders_fixed_rungs_are_exactly_two`).
- `ROLLER_DRY_RUN` returns before any placement; `ROLLER_ENABLED` unchanged.
- The credit invariant's four sites and `_CREDIT_EPSILON`.
- The evaluation's pricing-mode decision (imminence is decided once, at evaluation).
- `min_net_credit_per_contract` semantics ($/contract on the placed limits).

### DD-6 — The sim mirror: no adapter change; the identity moves; counts may drop and that is the finding, not a re-baseline

The replay drives the real `CallRoller` against `BacktestAlpacaClient` (FC-116 T3), so DD-3/DD-4 flow through without a second fill model. Under FC-116 D1 a buffered BTC limit is `≥ ask_c` → fills at `ask_c`, tag `limit_marketable`; a buffered STC limit is `≤ bid_c` → fills at `bid_c`. **Fills are identical to today; only eligibility changes** (the invariant is tested on buffered limits). The re-price path in the replay: a leg the book cannot fill is `expired` on the first read (no sleep), the re-price re-quotes the same daily book, produces the same limit, expires again — `time.sleep` must never be reached (T3's monkeypatch pins it; extend it to the re-price, T-12).

Consequences, stated: (i) both PRs touch `src/` → `ENGINE_VERSION`-independent identity moves (`engine_identity.compute_identity` hashes `src/`), every stored key re-replays once — PR-1 too, for a logging change; accept, and land PR-1 the same week as any other `src/` change if one is queued. (ii) PR-2's body tabulates `dip_then_recovering_window` and the GOOGL CC sim `a5c9c5626b4148eb` before/after: `rolls_executed` may **fall** by the marginal rolls DD-3 makes ineligible (`roll_skips.no_credit_candidate`-class reasons rise by the same count); `roll_net_credit` per executed roll is **unchanged**; `roll_legs_marketable == 2·rolls_executed`, `roll_legs_resting == 0` in base mode. A change in any *other* counter is a defect. *Rejected:* modelling the indicative-vs-NBBO offset in the adapter — there is no measurement of it yet (PR-1 produces the first), and FC-116 D1 explicitly refused to re-introduce a haircut under another name.

## Proposed approach — exact changes by file

### PR-1 (instrumentation; `[FC-120 PR-1]`)

## Risks & mitigations

- **Risk:** the buffer pays ~$10–18/contract of credit on every roll. **Mitigation:** it is the price of being marketable on a modified feed; PR-1's `fill_vs_limit` distribution sets the default (DD-1); `0.00` is a legal value; the invariant guarantees no debit.
- **Risk:** the re-price places a second live order after a cancel whose settle lied. **Mitigation:** DD-4 step 3 — a re-price is attempted only after a *terminal zero-fill* settle; `None` → unknown-disposition, unchanged; T-10(c).
- **Risk:** +15 s per-position worst case widens the FC-113 seam. **Mitigation:** the derived constant (DD-4, T-9); the cycle budget's 1500 s still fits two positions; FC-113 (b)/(c) unchanged in scope.
- **Risk:** identity churn — two `src/` PRs re-replay every stored sim key twice. **Mitigation:** DD-6 (ii) — accept; batch PR-1 with any queued `src/` change; the battery's pins prove the re-replay.
- **Risk:** `snap_limit` on an unverified root warns once per process (`_warn_if_unverified`) — fine on both profiles today (all configured roots verified, FC-072), but a new CC symbol outside the list would price at nickels above $3 by assumption. **Mitigation:** the warning event is already alert-visible (`tick_class_unverified`); add the root to `VERIFIED_PENNY_PROGRAM_ROOTS` when onboarding.
- **Risk:** the post-settle quote on the timeout path is a new network call in a 135 s-old thread near the cycle deadline. **Mitigation:** one call, try/except, after the disposition is final; the cycle's remaining budget is ≥ 15 s by construction of the per-position guard.
- **Risk:** Alpaca *live* would reject an off-tick limit (today's roller). **Mitigation:** PR-2 makes every roller limit tick-legal — recorded as a real-money precondition closed by this FC, alongside the OPRA one it does not close (Q1).
