## Context

The covered-call roller (FC-078, live on the CC service since 2026-09-17 via FC-100) prices its buy-to-close at `round(ask, 2)` and its sell-to-open at `round(bid, 2)` from `AlpacaClient.get_option_quote` (`call_roller.py:352-353`, `:526-544`), polls 120 s, then cancels. In its first week on the CC service the first leg filled **1 of 4** times. This plan establishes, from the full 30-day record on both services, what can and cannot be said about why — then specifies the instrumentation that makes the next miss diagnosable (PR-1) and the pricing rule that should stop most of them (PR-2).

**Five facts the plan rests on (rev 2 wording):**

1. **The log carries no quote.** `call_roll_btc_placed` / `call_roll_stc_placed` log `limit_price`, `contracts`, `pricing_mode` (BTC only) — not bid, ask, stamp, feed or sizes (`call_roller.py:610-617`, `:1034-1042`). `get_option_quote` already returns all of them (`alpaca_client.py:409-421`, the broker's `timestamp`); the roller drops them. Quote age at placement is unknowable for every event above. That gap is PR-1's deliverable.
2. **The feed is Alpaca's `indicative` feed** on both services (`OPTION_QUOTE_FEED`, `alpaca_client.py:48`; the OPRA agreement is unsigned — FC-072 §Rollout 0, `docs/CLAUDE.md:384-392`). Alpaca's description: *"trades are delayed and quotes are modified."* The endpoint is `get_option_latest_quote` — the latest quote, not a snapshot. The stock side is IEX, real-time (`get_stock_quote`, `alpaca_client.py:344-371`, with its own `timestamp`).
3. **Off-grid limits are the fingerprint of a modified quote, not the miss mechanism.** 12 of 19 BTC limits and 8 of 13 rung-1 limits were off the tick grid; an NBBO price cannot be, so those quotes were not NBBO prices. But on-grid misses exist (IWM 0.68 BTC 09-02; AMZN 5.50 rung 1 08-28) and off-grid limits filled 8/12 — the grid does not decide the fill. **Every one of the 26 fills was on the grid and strictly through its limit** (no fill at the limit): the placed limit was never the binding price; fills happen at the NBBO. Live consequence of an off-grid limit: Alpaca *"will attempt to route the order to the NOM. If they are not able to route the order to NOM, the order will be rejected"* (support article "Options Pricing Increments and Options Order Handling") — the same rule `limit_pricing.py:75-76` and FC-088 state. Paper accepted all of them; FC-072 added `round_to_tick` for the entry legs; the roller never calls it.
4. **Alpaca paper fills against the real NBBO** (paper-trading docs: matched against the NBBO; a buy limit fills only when `limit ≥ best ask`). So a BTC at the indicative ask fills only when the true ask is at or below it; the 13 improvements say the indicative ask sat 0.01–0.07 *above* the true ask; the five real-market misses say it sat *below* for 120 s. Whether that is a stale (delayed) quote or a transformed (modified) one, **the log cannot say** — fact 1. PR-1's `quote_age_s` (broker stamp vs. placement clock) is the delayed test; a same-second second read (`quote_reread_delta`) plus `cancel_quote_ask` is the modified test.
5. **Fills happen inside 25 s or not at all.** 26 fills ≤ 25 s; six 120 s waits produced nothing. Waiting longer is not the lever; being marketable at placement is — and if not, re-pricing early is (DD-4).

**Sim gap (FC-116 D1):** the replay fills a base-mode BTC at `min(limit, ask_c)` against a cent-quantised modeled book, so it can never miss — `roll_skips` post-placement terminals read 0 on lake chains by construction. The sim is not wrong about the rule; live is wrong about the quote.

**Log retention (rev 2, R5):** the CC service has **no BigQuery log sink** — the only sink is `options-wheel-logs` (filter `resource.labels.service_name="options-wheel-strategy" AND jsonPayload.event:*` → dataset `options_wheel_logs`; live: `run_googleapis_com_stderr_20260927` exists; `bq ls` shows datasets `covered_call`, `options_wheel`, `options_wheel_logs` only). `_Default` bucket retention is 30 d. ## Goals

- **PR-1 (S, no behaviour change):** every roll leg's placement, timeout/cancel and fill carries the quote it was priced from (bid/ask/mid/spread/sizes/broker stamp/age/feed), a same-second second read, the IEX stock quote and intrinsic at placement, the tick legality of the limit, the fresh quote at cancel time, the broker's fill price and fill latency — on the existing events *and* on one new per-leg terminal event (`call_roll_leg_settled`) that FC-113 and any later study can read as a table on both services (DD-9 gives the CC service a sink).
- **PR-2 (M):** base-mode limits are **marketable and tick-legal by construction** — BTC at `max(ask + buffer, parity + 0.01)` snapped *up*; STC at `bid − buffer` snapped *down*, never below the invariant floor — and a BTC that has not filled inside its window is **re-priced up to twice, at a pre-committed escalation, from a fresh quote**, inside the same 120 s budget. The credit invariant is tested on the buffered, snapped limits at every site, so a filled roll still cannot net a debit. FC-113 (a) derived per-position budget and (b) monotonic poll accounting land in the same PR.
- Both profiles, identical defaults (Symmetry; the profiles differ only in `itm_trigger_ratio`).
- The sim keeps driving the real roller: the rule change flows into the replay without a second fill model.

## Non-goals

- **Not the OPRA feed.** Signing the OPRA agreement / Algo Trader Plus is an operator purchase and a real-money gate (`docs/CLAUDE.md` §Real-money gates). PR-2's rule is feed-agnostic and remains correct on OPRA (one tick through the NBBO is the ordinary marketable limit); it is the paper-era mitigation, not a substitute. Q1 blocks the DD-1 gate *decision*, not the opening of either PR.
- Not the STC ladder's structure, rung count, naked-exposure terminals, cancel-then-settle, `_TERMINAL_ORDER_STATUSES`, or the one-live-order invariant. The STC ladder is not re-priced (DD-4).
- Not the schedule (15:30 ET; FC-078 DD-4) — Q4.
- Not FC-113 (c), the admission-time deadline / lock-wait bound (coordinate with FC-089).
- Not a new BigQuery table; a log-sink row is a table. The CC sink itself is an operator step (DD-9), not code.
- Not the imminence-mode pad value ($0.05) or threshold.
- Not `/monitor`'s `ask × 0.95` buy-to-close (FC-088's other half).

## Design decisions (with rejected alternatives)

### DD-1 — Two PRs, instrumentation first; PR-2 opens after 3 trading days of PR-1 data at the rev-2 defaults; the gate *confirms* the buffer, it no longer *sets* it

**Sequencing (R7):** PR-1 opens immediately. PR-2 opens after **≥ 3 trading days of PR-1 data** on both services (enough to read `quote_reread_delta` and `quote_age_s` — the two fields that decide whether the offset is per-read noise or a deterministic transform), built at the R1–R3 defaults below. The operator may override (Q2). PR-2 *merges* behind the gate decision.

**Gate rule (T4):** the readout is the **share of observed limit-vs-market offsets that fall inside the buffer**, on the 31-leg historical sample above joined with PR-1's rows (`fill_vs_limit` on fills; `quote_drift` / `cancel_quote_ask − quote_ask` on misses — the number the historical sample lacks). Today: 25/26 ≤ 0.10 (96 %); 20/26 ≤ 0.05 (77 %). The default **0.10 stands if ≥ 90 % of offsets are inside it**; otherwise it moves to the smallest tick-grid value that covers ≥ 90 %, within `[0, 0.50]`. The rule and the keys do not change either way; only the default might, and it moves by a yaml edit, not a rebuild. The 90 % threshold is the plan's choice (Q3, non-blocking).

**Q1 blocks the gate decision (R6):** the buffer default is feed-specific. If the operator will sign OPRA / Algo Trader Plus before real money, the gate is re-read on OPRA data before the first real-money roll (expect `0.00`–`0.05`); if not, the indicative feed is the standing condition and 0.10 is the number. The decision cannot be recorded without the answer; opening and reviewing PR-2 can.

*Rejected:* shipping both PRs at once — the defaults would rest on 31 legs with no cancel-time quote on any miss. *Rejected (rev 1):* "≥ 5 trading days or ≥ 4 placements, whichever later" — the roll rate on the CC service (one symbol) makes "4 placements" a month; the 3-day rule reads the two diagnostic fields, which every cycle produces (`call_roll_evaluated` re-reads too), not just placements.

### DD-2 — PR-1 field set (additive; no event renamed, no field removed; every value a JSON primitive)

Every field below is derived from data the roller already holds, one extra `get_option_quote` per placement (the second read) and one at cancel time. Names reuse FC-072's vocabulary (`quote_*`, `quote_age_s`, `quote_feed`). **S8:** every new field is a JSON primitive — `quote_ts = ts.isoformat()` (the broker stamp is a `datetime`, `alpaca_client.py:421`), `placed_at` ISO, `None` where absent; never a `datetime`, `Decimal` or dict. ### DD-3 — PR-2 pricing rule: buffer + parity floor + tick snap in base mode; imminence keeps its pad but is tick-snapped too

**Helper (T7 single signature):** `src/strategy/limit_pricing.py::snap_limit(price: Decimal, tick: Decimal, direction: Literal["up", "down"]) -> Decimal` — ceiling or floor to the tick grid; pure arithmetic, no tick lookup, no warning. The roller composes it: `tick = tick_size(price, underlying)` (tick from the **unsnapped** value, so the $3.00 boundary is decided once; `_warn_if_unverified` fires there), `float(snap_limit(price, tick, "up"))`. It is *not* `round_to_tick` (`:426`, which rounds sells **up** for the entry leg's never-above-the-ask bound); both stay, each docstring names the other. **S2:** every operand is `_dec(x)` (`limit_pricing.py:201-208`, `Decimal(str(float(x)))`); the buffer is `Decimal(str(buffer))`; no `Decimal(float)` anywhere in the roller.

**Base mode (R1, R3):**

```
buffer   = Decimal(str(config.rolling_marketable_buffer_per_share))     # default 0.10
parity   = _dec(stock_bid) - _dec(strike)                                # IEX stock bid, real-time (call_roller.py:245)
btc_raw  = max(_dec(ask) + buffer, parity + Decimal("0.01"))             # deep-ITM floor: marketable regardless of feed
btc      = snap_limit(btc_raw, tick_size(btc_raw, underlying), "up")
stc_raw  = _dec(bid) - buffer                                            # None if <= 0
stc      = snap_limit(stc_raw, tick_size(stc_raw, underlying), "down")
```

The parity term (R3) is feed-independent: a call's true ask cannot sit below intrinsic at the stock bid, so `parity + 0.01` is marketable whenever the indicative ask is quoted *below parity* — the modified-quote failure mode a buffer cannot bound. It binds only then (on the four CC events the evaluated extrinsics — 1.53 / 0.275 / 5.12 / 5.885 — put the ask above parity, so the buffered ask governed); PR-1's `limit_minus_intrinsic` measures how often it would have. The stock quote is the one the evaluation fetched (≤ 1 s old at the first placement); each re-price (DD-4) re-fetches it — on failure the parity term is omitted for that attempt, never a raise. `stock_bid`/`strike` ride on the opportunity dict (PR-1).

**Imminence mode:** `btc = snap_up(mid + 0.05)`, `stc = snap_down(mid − 0.05)` — the pad is unchanged, the buffer does **not** stack (imminence already crosses half the spread; FC-078 DD-2), the parity floor **does** apply to the BTC (it is a marketability floor, not a pad).

**STC rung 1 (R3) and rung 2:** in `_rungs` (`:952-978`), `floor = snap_up(_dec(btc_filled_price) + min_credit)` (rounds **up** — down would breach the invariant); rung 1 = `max(stc_buffered, floor)`; rung 2 = `floor`, yielded only when strictly below rung 1 (the existing `0 < floor_price < primary_limit` guard, `:974`). Stated plainly: when the BTC filled at or under its limit and rung 1's quote is the one the pre-BTC invariant was screened on, `stc_buffered ≥ floor` already and the `max` is a no-op; it binds only when the rung-1 quote is newer than the screen (a re-priced BTC refreshes it — DD-4 / S9) and the bid has fallen. The sell-at-cost rung 2 remains the floor; the expectation (fact 5 + the five rung-2 recoveries at 0.02–0.10 below rung 1) is that a rung 1 priced at `bid − 0.10` fills where the old rung 1 missed, so rung 2 is rarely reached. Rungs ≥ 3: base/imminence rule, then the invariant against the actual BTC fill, unchanged (`:990-991`).

**The invariant is tested on the buffered, snapped limits at every site:** the candidate screen (`_legal_candidates`, `:478`), the pre-BTC re-check (`:588-604`), each DD-4 re-price, order construction, every rung. Consequence: a candidate that netted less than `2·buffer + up to 2 ticks` (**$20–30/contract on a nickel-tick name at 0.10**) is no longer legal. **Buffer economics (T4):** the buffer is an *eligibility* bound, not a cost — fills happen at the NBBO (26/26 fills strictly through the limit), so a roll that fills nets the same credit it would have at the raw quote. None of the 13 completed rolls ($55–$325) would have been ineligible at 0.10 (the tightest, NVDA 09-08, placed 5.47 / 5.95 → buffered 5.60 / 5.85 → +$25 ≥ the $0 floor).

*Why not the deleted percentage pads (FC-078 DD-1)?* They were applied *after* the invariant was screened on raw quotes, so a pad could push a screened-legal pair into a debit. Here the buffer is applied *before* every invariant test; it can only make a marginal roll ineligible. Flat dollars anchored to the tick, not a percentage of a modified quote.

*Rejected — 0.05 default (rev 1):* covers 20/26 observed offsets (77 %); 0.10 covers 25/26 (96 %), and the one it misses was favourable. *Rejected — spread-scaled `max(0.10, k·spread)`:* the indicative spread is itself a modified number (fact 3); add the term if PR-1 shows the misses scale with the quoted spread. *Rejected — a longer timeout:* fact 5 (26 fills ≤ 25 s; none 30–120 s). *Rejected — a different endpoint:* `get_option_latest_quote` *is* the latest quote; the feed, not the endpoint, is the cause. *Deferred — expiry-day rolls before 15:30:* Q4.

### DD-4 — PR-2 BTC re-price: a pre-committed escalation, up to two re-prices inside the same 120 s, each after a terminal zero-fill settle; `rejected` is terminal; the STC ladder is not re-priced

`btc_fill_timeout_seconds` (120) is the BTC leg's **total** poll budget, split evenly across `btc_reprice_attempts + 1` windows (default **2 → 40 s / 40 s / 40 s**; the data says every fill lands ≤ 25 s, so a 40 s window forfeits nothing). Attempt `n` (n = 0, 1, 2) is priced at **`ask_fresh + (n+1)·buffer`** (then the parity floor and the snap, DD-3) — a committed ladder, not "the default might change". Sequence in `execute_roll`, extracted as `_place_and_settle_btc(opportunity) -> Tuple[Optional[Dict], Dict]`:

1. Place attempt `n`; `_poll_order_fill(order_id, timeout=window)` (`window = timeout // (attempts + 1)`).
2. Terminal fill (full or partial) → today's path, unchanged.
3. `status == "rejected"` → **terminal `btc_rejected`** (`:660-672`), never re-priced (R2): a rejection is the broker's verdict on the order, not on the price.
4. Poll returns `None` → `_cancel_and_settle` (15 s). Settle `None` → `_unknown_disposition` (`:858`), **no second order, ever**. Settle with `filled_qty > 0` → the ladder (a cancel that lost the race is a fill). Settle terminal zero-fill and `n < attempts` → step 5. Terminal zero-fill from the poll itself (`expired` / `canceled` — the replay's path, `alpaca_adapter.py:607-640`) → step 5 without a cancel.
5. **Re-price:** fresh `get_option_quote(old_symbol)` and fresh `get_stock_quote(underlying)`; recompute the limit by DD-3 at `(n+2)·buffer` (**same pricing mode as the evaluation** — imminence is decided once); fresh `get_option_quote(new_symbol)` → fresh STC limit by DD-3, which **replaces `opportunity['stc_limit']`, `fallback_candidates[0]['stc_limit']` and the rung-1 quote exactly as `:605-607` does today** (S9); re-run the pre-BTC invariant check. Invariant fails → terminal skip `credit_gone_at_reprice` (a new `skip_reason`, sibling of `credit_gone_at_execution`; nothing is live). Option quote unusable (`ask ≤ 0`) → `call_roll_btc_timeout_canceled` with `reprice_skipped_reason="btc_quote_unavailable"`. Otherwise emit `call_roll_btc_repriced` (`attempt`, `prior_limit`, `new_limit`, `prior_order_id`, both quote sets, `stock_bid`) and place; back to step 1 with `n + 1`. Exhausted → `call_roll_btc_timeout_canceled` as today, with `attempts` on it.
6. Every placement emits its own `call_roll_leg_settled` row (`attempt = n`) — a leg that was re-priced twice produces **three** rows.

The STC ladder is *not* re-priced: it has a retry structure (rung 2 at the floor, rungs 3+ on other candidates), each rung's quote is fetched when reached, and rung 2 is far through the book by construction (5/5 filled at the first poll). *Rejected:* re-pricing rung 1 — it would add windows to the naked interval a rung the floor already covers.

Same signature, same return contract. One `src` PR, one identity move fewer.

### DD-6 — The sim mirror: no adapter change; the identity moves; base-mode fills identical, imminence fills move ≤ 1 tick; expected multiplicity stated

The replay drives the real `CallRoller` against `BacktestAlpacaClient` (FC-116 T3), so DD-3/DD-4 flow through without a second fill model. Under FC-116 D1 (`alpaca_adapter.py:547-602`):

- **Base mode: fills identical to today.** A buffered BTC limit is `≥ ask_c` → fills at `ask_c`, tag `limit_marketable`; a buffered STC limit is `≤ bid_c` → fills at `bid_c`. Only eligibility changes (the invariant is tested on buffered limits). `roll_net_credit` per executed roll is unchanged; `roll_legs_marketable == 2·rolls_executed`, `roll_legs_resting == 0`. **In base mode, a change in any other counter is a defect.**

Expected multiplicity: **2 orders in `client.get_orders()`, 1 ledger `buy_to_close` row, `roll_legs_marketable` +1, `call_roll_btc_repriced` ×1, `call_roll_leg_settled` ×2, `time.sleep` never reached.** A third order is unreachable in the replay (the re-priced limit fills or, on a one-sided book, `btc_quote_unavailable` ends the leg). ## Risks & mitigations

- **Risk:** the buffer makes marginal rolls ineligible (`2·buffer + ≤ 2 ticks`, $20–30/contract on nickel names). **Mitigation:** it costs nothing on fills (NBBO); none of the 13 completed rolls would have been excluded; DD-1's gate rule can lower it; `0.00` is legal; the invariant guarantees no debit.
- **Risk:** the re-price places a second live order after a cancel whose settle lied. **Mitigation:** DD-4 step 4 — only after a *terminal zero-fill* settle; `None` → unknown-disposition; `rejected` → terminal; T-10(c)/(h).
- **Risk:** +30 s per-position worst case (705 vs 675). **Mitigation:** the derived budget (DD-4, T-9); 1410 s for two positions ≤ 1500; the seam invariant at 1560 ≤ 1800; FC-113 (c) unchanged in scope.
- **Risk:** identity churn — two `src/` PRs re-replay every stored sim key twice. **Mitigation:** DD-6 (i); batch PR-1 with any queued `src/` change; the battery's pins prove the re-replay.
- **Risk:** `snap_limit` composition on an unverified root warns once per process and prices at nickels above $3 by assumption. **Mitigation:** `tick_class_unverified` is alert-visible; add the root to `VERIFIED_PENNY_PROGRAM_ROOTS` when onboarding. **Periodic `ppind` re-verification (T-LOW):** penny-program membership changes; a root that leaves it prices at nickels below $3 and is *rejected* live, not routed. Quarterly operator check, recorded as a real-money gate row (R6).
- **Risk:** the post-settle quote and the second read are new network calls near the cycle deadline. **Mitigation:** try/except, `None` on failure, after the disposition is final; the per-position guard reserves the full worst case.
- **Risk — live tick handling (T2, corrected):** Alpaca live routes a non-conforming limit to NOM and **rejects only if it cannot**; the roller's `btc_rejected` / `stc_rejected` paths exist but have never fired on paper. PR-2 makes the roller's limits tick-legal, which **removes the roller from FC-088's gate row — it does not by itself clear a gate**: live rejected-path handling and exchange price protection are separate rows (R6). Rev 1's "closes a real-money precondition" was an over-claim.
- **Risk — rung-2 price protection is a live divergence:** rung 2 sits 20–60 % through the NBBO (5 paper fills at 3.05/3.50/0.65/1.69/6.90 against bids of 4.75/5.40/1.58/3.15/8.95); Cboe / NOM limit-order price protection can reject or re-price it live. **Mitigation:** gate row (R6); the ladder already treats `stc_rejected` as "place the next rung"; R3's rung 1 at `bid − 0.10` is meant to fill before rung 2 is needed; verify on the first live roll.
- **Risk:** the Mock fixtures make every roll test fail at once if a new config attribute is missed. **Mitigation:** PR-2 item 8 lists the exact attributes; `Mock() // 2` raising is the tripwire, not a surprise.
