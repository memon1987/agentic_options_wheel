## Context — what rev 1's reviews established (all verified against the journal + Alpaca)

- The old call limit `round(premium × 0.95, 2)` was not "5% below mid" in effect: on the typical call book (mid $1.30, spread $0.13) 5% of mid ≈ half a spread, so the limit sat **≈ at the bid**. Realized Σ(mid − fill) over the 66 journaled call fills = **−$87** — a below-bid sell fills at the current bid anyway. The "$2.35k left on the table" was Σ(mid − limit), not money.
- **The dominant variable is quote staleness.** `/scan` quotes at :00, `/run` places at :15, and nothing on the execute path re-quotes. Of 66 filled calls, 17 filled below the scan-time bid and 28 at/above scan-time mid (median time-to-fill 0.13 s — marketable against the *current* book). Pricing "at mid" off a 15-minute-old quote skips the writes where the stock drifted down and fills the up-drift ones at the then-current bid: selection, not spread capture.
- **Fill-rate baseline for at-mid is ~75–80%**, not the 87% at-bid figure (position-in-book table: calls ≤ bid 90.6%, puts == mid 75%, puts > mid+10%spread 67%).
- **Alpaca's tick rule** (support doc "Options Pricing Increments"): $0.01 below $3.00, **$0.05 at/above $3.00**, except penny-program names (SPY/QQQ/IWM penny at all prices); non-conforming limits are routed to NOM if possible, otherwise **rejected**. The paper simulator does not enforce increments (46 off-tick ≥$3 orders "accepted", 34 of 38 fills snapped to a 5c tick on the fill side) — the "0 rejected" evidence is paper-only.
- The rev-1 spread cap made the *put* leg concede more on wide books (1/118 historical), and the locked-book fallback priced 5% through a locked market on both legs. Half-cent mids rounded by float luck.
- `AlpacaClient.get_option_quote` exists and is already used at execute time by the roller and `/monitor`.

## Design decisions (made — do not relitigate in the build)

1. **Re-quote at execute time, both legs (Symmetry).** In `execute_call_sale` / `execute_put_sale`, immediately before pricing, call `self.alpaca.get_option_quote(option_symbol)`; if it returns a usable `bid`/`ask` (per the predicate below) use it and set `quote_source="live"`, `quote_age_s=0`; otherwise fall back to the opportunity's `bid`/`ask`/`premium` with `quote_source="blob"` and `quote_age_s = now − opportunity['scanned_at']` (the scanner stamps `scanned_at`; if absent, `None`). Never fail the order because the re-quote failed — log `quote_refresh_failed` and fall back. **Mid = (bid + ask) / 2 from whichever source is used**, recomputed — never the blob `premium` when a live quote is present.
2. **Limit formula (shared `src/strategy/limit_pricing.py`, both legs):**
   `limit = mid + f × spread` where `f = call_limit_spread_fraction` (calls, config, default **0.0**) or the put side's existing `0.10` (`put_limit_spread_fraction`, new config key, default 0.10 so put behavior is unchanged on a normal book).
3. **Quote-usability predicate** (`quote_is_usable`): `bid > 0 and ask > 0 and ask >= bid`. **Locked book (`ask == bid`)** is usable and prices **at bid (== ask)** — never through the market. Wide book (`spread / mid > 0.5`): **usable, no cap** — price by the leg's normal formula (rev 1's cap-then-discount conceded more where the mid was least informative; a wide two-sided book is illiquid, not stale). One-sided (`bid == 0` or `ask == 0`) or crossed (`ask < bid`) → unusable → fallback `round(premium × 0.95, 2)` exactly as before rev 1, with `spread_fraction=None` in the log.
4. **Tick snapping (sell side, round UP — never give away a tick):** if `limit >= 3.00` and the underlying is not in `PENNY_PROGRAM_SYMBOLS = {"SPY", "QQQ", "IWM"}`, snap to the next $0.05 (`math.ceil(limit / 0.05) × 0.05`, then `round(…, 2)`). Below $3.00 round to $0.01. **Half-cent rule:** below $3, round half **up** (`Decimal` quantize `ROUND_HALF_UP`, not `round()`), so a 1c-wide book at mid x.xx5 prices at the ask (rests) rather than the bid — pin with a test. Do not snap the *fallback* below $3 differently from the main path.
5. **Log fields on `call_sale_executing` / `put_sale_executing`:** `bid`, `ask`, `mid`, `spread`, `spread_fraction`, `limit_price`, `quote_source`, `quote_age_s`, `tick_snapped` (bool). These make the two-week study computable from logs: fill-rate by `quote_source` × pricing mode, and realized (fill − mid).
6. **Docs/economics corrected everywhere** (plan §Context above is the source): remove every "~$2.3k" / "5% donated" claim from `call_seller.py`, `limit_pricing.py`, `docs/CLAUDE.md`; state the at-mid expectation (~75–80% fills) and that the recoverable amount is the realized (fill − mid) delta, expected ≈ +$5/write gross.
7. **Roller asymmetry stated:** `call_roller.py` prices its STO at the bid (or mid − $0.05 on imminence) on purpose (credit-only defensive rolls); one sentence in `limit_pricing.py`'s docstring and `docs/CLAUDE.md` — not routed through this module.
8. **`opportunity['scanned_at']`:** add to both scanner opportunity dicts (`options_scanner.py` put + call construction) as ISO-8601 UTC from `clock.now()`; the `/run` age computation uses it. Missing → `quote_age_s=None`.

## Behavior contract

- Live quote usable → limit from the **fresh** mid; blob quote used only on refresh failure/unusable, logged as such.
- Normal put book (`bid < ask`, both > 0, live or blob): limit identical to pre-rev-1 `round(mid + 0.10 × spread, 2)` **except** for tick snapping ≥ $3 (new, intended) — pin on a fixture below $3 that the put limit is byte-identical to the historical formula.
  **Carve-out accepted in review (2026-08-28):** on a **one-tick-wide** book (spread == the class tick) both legs price at the bid — there is no mid on such a book, and resting a DAY order at the ask risks a cycle of theta for one cent. On the put leg this changes 1c-wide books from the ask to the bid: 5 of 118 journaled put rows (4.2%; NVDA ×3, IWM ×2), −$1/contract. Every other put book below $3 is byte-identical (16,409-book brute force: all diffs are the 1c-wide books).
- Calls: limit = mid (f = 0.0) with tick/half-cent rules.
- Locked book → at bid. One-tick book → at bid (both legs). Wide book → normal formula. One-sided/crossed → fallback.
- Order type/TIF/qty, cost-basis floor ordering, buying-power ordering, wrong-seller rejection: unchanged. Re-quote happens **after** the floor check (a refreshed quote must not bypass the floor).
- Backtest replay: no network re-quote; results byte-identical to main for the same window (prove by running one window on main and on the branch).
