## Context

### The bug, re-verified for this plan (2026-07-29 00:32 ET, live probe against Alpaca)

`GapDetector._get_previous_close` (`src/risk/gap_detector.py:526-563`) fetches daily bars
(`get_stock_bars(symbol, days=5)`, line 541), filters `df[df.index < current_time]`
(line 551), and returns `.iloc[-1]` (line 555). Alpaca stamps daily stock bars at
**midnight ET** — 04:00 UTC under EDT, 05:00 UTC under EST — far before any decision time
(9:35 ET = 13:35 UTC). The current session's own bar therefore passes the filter, and
"previous close" is **today's close**.

Live probe (NVDA, simulated 9:35 ET decision on 2026-07-28):

- Bars returned stamped `2026-07-28 04:00:00+00:00`, index dtype `datetime64[ns, UTC]`.
- `df[df.index < 2026-07-28T13:35Z]` **includes the 2026-07-28 bar**.
- `_get_previous_close("NVDA", ...)` → **197.01** (the session's own close).
  Correct answer: **196.51** (2026-07-27, the prior session).
- The date-based pattern (below) returns 196.51. Fix pattern verified.

### The precise failure mode — it does not read as a 0.0% gap

During the session, `get_stock_bars` serves a **partial** bar for the current day (the
live client fetches with `end = now − 20min` for the SIP delay, `src/api/alpaca_client.py:391`),
so its `close` is a ~20-minutes-ago print. `can_execute_trade` (gap_detector.py:438-524)
compares a **real-time IEX quote** (alpaca_client.py:296-313) against it. The gate thus
measures ~20 minutes of drift, not the overnight gap. Observed drifts on record ran
−0.967% to +1.212% — nonzero, so `gap_percent` in the logs looks alive. On-record
evidence: today's bar returned in 10/10 live trials (NVDA, AAPL), FC entry dated 2026-07-17.

**Correction to the on-record evidence.** The FC-036 entry claims "3 of 5" sampled NVDA
days should have blocked against `execution_gap_threshold = 1.5`. The five listed gaps are
−2.295%, −1.096%, +0.076%, +2.295%, −1.147% — exactly **2 of 5** exceed 1.5 in absolute
value. The qualitative conclusion (real overnight gaps that should have blocked did not)
stands; the "3 of 5" figure must not be propagated. The study recomputes exact rates from
data. This plan's own probe found 2 of the last 7 NVDA sessions (2026-07-20 +1.509%,
2026-07-21 +2.096%) above 1.5 — the gate will fire regularly once fixed.

### The working pattern to follow

`_detect_current_gap` (gap_detector.py:186-246) compares **dates**, not timestamps:

- line 204: `target_date = date.date()`
- line 207: `df_dates = pd.Series([idx.date() for idx in df.index], index=df.index)`
- line 214: `previous_data = df.loc[df_dates < target_date].iloc[-1]`

This is Stage 2's current-day gap check and it is correct: `.date()` of a midnight-ET
stamp (04:00 or 05:00 UTC) is always the trading date, in both DST regimes. The fix ports
this pattern into `_get_previous_close`. Verified against live data in the probe above.

### Blast radius (verified by grep at d493d46)

- `_get_previous_close` has exactly **one** caller: `can_execute_trade` (line 461).
- `can_execute_trade` has two callers:
  `WheelEngine._find_new_opportunities` (`src/strategy/wheel_engine.py:345`) — Stage 4,
  gating **both new puts and new covered calls** per symbol per cycle — and
  `GapDetector.get_execution_delay_recommendation` (gap_detector.py:575), which has **no
  callers anywhere** (dead code; do not expand scope to remove it, but do not reason from it).
- `run_rolling_cycle` (wheel_engine.py:1385) does **not** consult Stage 4 → rolls unaffected.
- Live cadence: three scheduled scans/day, ~9:35 / 12:00 / 15:00 ET
  (`docs/investigations/fc-032-parity-check.md`, Method + premium sections).

### Relationship to the other gap control — do not treat them as independent

Stage 2 (`filter_stocks_by_gap_risk` → `analyze_gap_risk`: 30-day gap-frequency ≤ 15% of
days with >2% moves, plus the 40% realized-vol cap) **works today** and is currently the
only functioning gap control; it blocks whole symbols for weeks (NVDA ~90% of Jun–Jul 2026).
FC-042 Track B1 is studying whether to **loosen** it (FC-002). Sequencing consequence:

- This fix should merge **before** any FC-002 live change, so a loosened Stage 2 inherits
  a working per-day Stage-4 backstop.
- B1's engine arms must run on **post-fix** code with the study's explicit threshold
  override — otherwise B1's "filter off" arm has zero gap control of any kind and
  overstates the risk of loosening Stage 2.
- Reviewers must assess the **net risk of the pair** (Stage 4 arming while Stage 2 may
  loosen), not each change alone.

### Why the backtest replay reproduced the bug (and what that means here)

`BacktestAlpacaClient.get_stock_bars` (`src/backtesting/engine/alpaca_adapter.py:225-264`)
deliberately mirrors live: bars stamped 04:00 UTC (`_BAR_STAMP`, lines 48-55), the
simulated day's own bar included, index tz-aware UTC. The sim decision timestamp is 16:00
(SimClock, `src/backtesting/engine/clock.py:26`). So in a replay the broken gate computes
`(close_D − close_D)/close_D = exactly 0.0%` and **never fires** — the replay reproduces
the *never-blocks* consequence faithfully (it cannot reproduce the live premarket-drift
noise, which needs intraday prints). `RejectionTally` already maps `stage_4_blocked` →
"execution gap check (stage 4)" (`src/backtesting/engine/rejections.py:33`), so blocked-day
counts fall out of every replay for free.

## Design decisions (made; do not relitigate during build)

**D1 — Client-side date filter, following the Stage-2 pattern.**
Rejected alternatives:
- *Request bars with explicit `end = yesterday`* (server-side): changes the shared
  `get_stock_bars` surface for one caller, drags the SIP-delay end-date logic into the
  contract, and forces a matching adapter change. More blast radius, same result.
- *Extract a shared `_bars_before_date` helper used by both Stage 2 and Stage 4*: DRY-nice
  but touches the one working gap control in a live-risk PR for zero behavior change.
  Acceptable as a later refactor-only PR.
- *Rebuild the gate on the true overnight gap (open_D vs close_D−1)*: the session open is
  not available from the 20-min-delayed daily bar at the 9:35 scan (the partial bar ends
  ~9:15, before the open), so this needs a different data source and changes the gate's
  contract at midday scans. Out of scope.

**D2 — Semantics stay "current IEX quote vs previous session's close".** That is the
code's documented contract; only the previous-close input was wrong. Consequence: at 9:35
the gate ≈ overnight gap; at 12:00/15:00 it includes intraday drift — a strictly stronger
check later in the day. A day is fully lost only if **all three** scans measure >threshold.
The study quantifies both bounds; the threshold value is a study output (D5).

**D3 — Backtest adapter code is unchanged.** The adapter mirrors the *data layer* (bar
stamps, today's-bar inclusion), which is not the bug; the fixed detector runs unmodified
in replay and date-filters today's bar in both worlds (sim: 16:00 naive → `.date()` = D →
prior bar = D−1 ✓). The replay's residual infidelity — the fixed gate in replay measures
**close-to-close** (quote = close at the single 16:00 decision), not the 9:35 overnight
gap — is an engine-convention bias, quantified by the study's misclassification matrix.
Probe example of why this matters: NVDA 2026-07-27 moved −4.99% close-to-close (replay
gate blocks) but gapped only +0.66% overnight (live 9:35 gate passes). Update
**comments/docstrings only**: adapter lines 229-234 (and the module note at 48-55) which
describe the old GapDetector inclusion behavior, and the rationale text of
`test_stock_bars_index_is_tz_aware_utc_like_live` (tests/test_backtest_adapter.py:85-100 —
its assertions stay).

**Comparability of prior backtest results.** `parity_check.py` is unaffected (it drives
`MarketDataManager.find_suitable_puts` directly; Stage 4 is not in its path). Full
replays and `--command screen` runs are affected the moment the default-config threshold
arms: symbols with frequent >threshold close-to-close days will trade less than in any
pre-fix report. `bq_writer.py` already persists `execution_gap_threshold` per run
(src/backtesting/reporting/bq_writer.py:120), so runs remain distinguishable. Rule going
forward: any cross-run comparison must pin the threshold explicitly; the study harness
always sets it in the injected `Config` rather than relying on `settings.yaml`.

**D4 — No new config flag; phased rollout on the existing knob.** A new boolean would be
permanent config debt and a second way for this gate to be silently off — the exact
failure mode FC-036 exists to kill. `execution_gap_threshold` itself is the flag:
- **Phase A (fix PR):** merge the code fix with `execution_gap_threshold: 999` in
  `config/settings.yaml`, loudly commented as FC-036 shadow mode. The gate runs correct
  math in production, logs true `gap_percent` on every `stage_4_passed` event, blocks
  nothing. 5 trading days of live 9:35/12:00/15:00 measurements — the only data that can
  validate the study's EOD proxy against reality (the study cannot reconstruct intraday
  prints historically).
- **Phase B (arm PR):** one-line config change to the study-selected threshold, with the
  shadow-week distribution vs. study prediction in the PR description.
Rollback at any point is a one-line config revert (minutes via the normal deploy), not a
code revert. `enable_gap_detection: false` also exists but kills Stage 2 too — documented,
not the lever. Note: during Phase A, default-config replays also run at 999, so backtest
behavior is unchanged until Phase B — studies must pass explicit overrides (they do).

**D5 — Threshold value is a study output.** 1.5 has never been validated: it was set when
the gate was dead and has never blocked a real trade. Sweep {1.5, 2.5, 3.5, 5.0}; decision
rules pre-registered below.

### Layer 1 — measurement, no engine

Per symbol-day over the window, from provider daily bars:
1. **Overnight gap** = (open_D − close_D−1)/close_D−1 — what a 9:35-adjacent gate measures.
2. **Close-to-close move** = (close_D − close_D−1)/close_D−1 — what the EOD-replayed gate
   measures.
3. Rates of |each| > {1.5, 2.5, 3.5, 5.0}; distribution stats.
4. **Misclassification matrix at 1.5** between the two measures (replay-blocks vs
   9:35-would-block, both/neither) — this is the honesty instrument for reading Layer 2.
5. **Live-fills join (highest-value number in the study):** join per-day gaps against real
   entry fills from BQ `options_wheel.trades_from_activities` (sell-to-open put/call
   dates, 2024-02→present). For every real entry on a day where |overnight gap| > 1.5:
   what did that specific trade realize (premium kept / assigned, and the eventual cycle
   P&L via the activities for that contract; fallback attribution if the join is gnarly:
   strike vs expiry-date close from bars)? Output: **dollars of actually-taken trades a
   working 1.5-gate would have prevented, and whether they won or lost.**
6. The FC-entry corrected sample: recompute the five cited NVDA days, report 2/5.

**Spurious-fire rate of the current broken gate:** not historically reconstructible (needs
9:15-vs-9:35 intraday prints). Bound it honestly: the 10-trial live sample (drift −0.967%
to +1.212%, i.e. 0/10 would have spuriously fired at 1.5) plus the Phase-A shadow week,
which measures the true live distribution. Say exactly this in the doc; no fake precision.

### Pre-registered decision rules (what would argue AGAINST shipping)

Evidence **against arming at 1.5** — any of:
- (a) The blocked real trades (Layer 1 #5) were **net profitable** by >5% of window
  premium income, with no offsetting tail-loss reduction: the gate only costs money.
- (b) Engine arms show return-on-collateral monotonically improving as the threshold
  rises, with no drawdown/worst-cycle improvement at tighter thresholds: the gate adds
  nothing at any sweep level.
- (c) The 9:35-measure block rate at 1.5 exceeds ~25-30% of decision days on NVDA/AMD:
  the gate is a de-facto symbol embargo duplicating Stage 2 — wrong tool for the risk.

Disposition under (a)/(b): **merge the code fix anyway** (a gate that lies about what it
measures is not a control — correctness is not threshold-dependent), but Phase B arms at a
higher swept threshold or holds at 999 pending a joint FC-002 decision; surfaced to owner
with the numbers. Under (c): recommend the lowest swept threshold with a block rate under
~10-15%. Evidence **for 1.5**: blocked trades net-negative (especially assignment-heavy),
tail metrics improve, block rate materially under 15%.

**Bias footer (required in the study doc):** single vol regime (2024+); EOD close-to-close
proxy vs live 9:35 measure (matrix quantifies; replay overstates full-day blocking because
a 9:35 block can pass at 12:00/15:00 and the replay has one decision/day); replay premium
understatement ~20% (parity doc) applies to arm P&L; NVDA window starts post-split.

## Half 2 — The fix

### Exact change

`src/risk/gap_detector.py`, `_get_previous_close` (lines 526-563). Signature unchanged:
`def _get_previous_close(self, symbol: str, current_time: datetime) -> Optional[float]`.
New body (behavior-normative; cosmetics free):

```python
try:
    df = self.alpaca.get_stock_bars(symbol, days=10)
    if df.empty:
        return None
    # Date-based, not timestamp-based (FC-036): Alpaca stamps daily bars at
    # midnight ET (04:00/05:00 UTC), so a timestamp comparison admits the
    # current session's own partial bar at any intraday decision time.
    # Mirrors _detect_current_gap's pattern (Stage 2).
    target_date = current_time.date()
    df_dates = pd.Series([idx.date() for idx in df.index], index=df.index)
    prior = df.loc[df_dates < target_date]
    if prior.empty:
        return None
    return prior['close'].iloc[-1]
except Exception as e:
    # unchanged: same log event (previous_close_error), return None
```

Also, inside this function only:
- **Delete** the dead `lookback_date = current_time - timedelta(days=3)` (line 538 — never
  used).
- **Delete** the pytz localize block (lines 546-548) — no timestamp comparison remains;
  only `.date()` is used, valid for naive and aware inputs alike.
- **`days=5` → `days=10`:** a calendar-day lookback of 5 can span 4+ consecutive
  non-trading days (holiday clusters, emergency closures) and leave no prior-session bar;
  10 always spans ≥2 sessions for any US closure on record. Same single API call.

Docstring: state the contract — "close of the last trading day strictly before
`current_time`'s calendar date; None if no such bar is available (caller fails closed)."

`config/settings.yaml:150`: `execution_gap_threshold: 1.5` → `999` with comment
`# FC-036 Phase A shadow — gate fixed but not armed; Phase B sets the study threshold. See docs/plans/fc-036.md`.

Comment-only updates (no behavior): `src/backtesting/engine/alpaca_adapter.py` lines
48-55 and 225-243 docstrings; `tests/test_backtest_adapter.py:85-100` docstring.

### Behavior contract

- **Inputs:** `symbol`; `current_time` naive or tz-aware. Production passes `clock.now()`
  (naive; UTC on Cloud Run). Date semantics: the bar-exclusion date is
  `current_time.date()` in `current_time`'s own frame; bar dates come from the index's
  own tz (midnight-ET stamps → `.date()` = trading date under both EDT and EST).
- **Output:** previous session's close as float, or `None` (df empty, or no bar strictly
  before `current_time`'s date). `can_execute_trade` maps `None` →
  `{'can_execute': False, 'reason': 'no_previous_close_data'}` — **fail-closed, unchanged**.
- **Edge cases:**
  - *Weekend/holiday/long weekend:* Tuesday-after-Monday-holiday returns Friday's close
    (no Monday bar exists — date filter is naturally calendar-aware).
  - *First trading day of available data:* no prior bar → None → blocked (fail-closed).
    In replay this cannot occur on decision days — the simulator loads 60 warm-up
    calendar days before `start` (simulator.py:158-160).
  - *Session's bar absent at 9:35* (thin symbol, no premarket prints): date filter is a
    no-op and the last bar is already yesterday's — same correct answer.
  - *DST boundaries:* EDT stamps (04:00 UTC) and EST stamps (05:00 UTC) both `.date()` to
    the trading date; transition Sundays are non-trading. Covered by tests.
  - *Half-days:* daily bars are stamped and dated identically on early closes; no special
    handling.
  - *Post-close, pre-8pm-ET call:* still returns yesterday's close (same date all
    session). *After 8pm ET with a UTC clock,* `.date()` rolls to tomorrow and the
    function returns **today's** close — i.e., "most recent completed close", which is
    the correct gap reference for any subsequent decision. No live scan runs in that
    window (scans ~9:35/12:00/15:00 ET); pinned by a test as documented semantics.
  - *Split days (live, future):* raw bars mean the day after a split measures a ~−90%
    "gap" and blocks the symbol for that day. Same property Stage 2 already has;
    acceptable (entering during contract-adjustment chaos is not desirable). Note in the
    fix PR description.
  - *Bad IEX quote (zero bid/ask):* unchanged — `can_execute_trade` computes a huge
    |gap| and fails closed. This is pre-existing behavior, not introduced here; the
    shadow week will show its real frequency.
- **What must NOT change (regression contract):**
  - Stage 2 behavior and all its functions — byte-identical.
  - `can_execute_trade`'s return shape and reason strings (`gap_detection_disabled`,
    `no_previous_close_data`, `execution_gap_exceeded`, `gap_within_limits`,
    `gap_check_error`) — RejectionTally and the dashboard depend on the event stream.
  - `get_stock_bars` (live client and adapter): signatures, stamping, inclusion of the
    current session's bar. The **data layer stays a faithful mirror**; only the detector's
    interpretation changes.
  - Roll cycle: no Stage-4 consult, before or after.
  - Log events emitted by wheel_engine Stage 4 (`stage_4_blocked` / `stage_4_passed`).

## Test requirements

New `tests/test_gap_detector.py` (unit; construct `GapDetector` with a mock config in
**percent units** and a mock client returning crafted frames):

1. **Midnight-stamp regression (the test that would have caught FC-036):** frame with
   bars stamped 04:00 UTC including the decision date's own bar; decision 13:35 UTC same
   date → asserts return is *yesterday's* close, not today's. Repeat with 05:00 UTC
   stamps (EST). *Catches:* reintroduction of any timestamp-vs-index comparison.
2. **Gate-level behavior:** `can_execute_trade` with mocked quote 2% above previous close,
   threshold 1.5 → blocked with `reason='execution_gap_exceeded'` and correct
   `gap_percent`; at 1% → passes. *Catches:* percent/fraction units regressions and
   return-shape drift.
3. **DST boundary:** Friday bar stamped 04:00 UTC (EDT) + Monday bar stamped 05:00 UTC
   (EST) across a November transition; decision Monday 14:35 UTC (9:35 EST) → Friday's
   close. Mirror for March. *Catches:* any future stamp-hour assumption.
4. **Long weekend/holiday:** Thu+Fri bars, no Monday bar, decision Tuesday → Friday's
   close... (bars Thu, Fri, Tue-partial; asserts Fri). *Catches:* off-by-one "previous
   calendar day" logic.
5. **No prior bar:** only the decision date's bar in frame → `None`; and via
   `can_execute_trade` → `can_execute=False`, `reason='no_previous_close_data'`.
   *Catches:* fail-open regressions on missing data.
6. **Empty frame** → `None`. **Exception from client** → `None` + `previous_close_error`
   logged (assert no raise). *Catches:* fail-closed contract.
7. **Post-close semantics:** aware decision 21:00 ET (01:00 UTC next day) → returns the
   just-completed session's close; documented in the test as intended "most recent
   completed close" semantics. *Catches:* accidental "return None after hours".

## Risks & mitigations

- **Risk:** arming blocks a material fraction of entries (NVDA close-to-close >1.5% is
  common at 40% vol — probe week: 4/7 days; overnight measure 2/7).
  **Mitigation:** the study's decision rules + sweep pick the threshold; Phase A/B split
  means arming is a deliberate, evidenced, one-line change; block-rate prediction is
  checked against the shadow week before arming.
- **Risk:** the EOD study over/under-states live blocking (close-to-close ≠ 9:35 measure).
  **Mitigation:** misclassification matrix (Layer 1 #4) + shadow-week live distribution
  are both required before Phase B.
- **Risk:** fixing Stage 4 while B1 loosens Stage 2 changes net gap risk in ways neither
  study sees alone. **Mitigation:** sequencing rule (this fix merges first; B1 runs
  post-fix with explicit overrides); reviewers directed to assess the pair.
- **Risk:** Phase B never happens and 999 becomes the new dead gate.
  **Mitigation:** FC-036 stays open until Phase B merges; the plan's Execution section
  tracks both PRs; the settings.yaml comment names the plan.
- **Risk:** covered-call entries are also gated (Stage 4 sits above both sides), so
  post-assignment shares can sit uncovered on gap days.
  **Mitigation:** intended per the symmetry principle, but it is a behavior change on the
  call side — stated here, measured in the study (blocked-day counts are per-symbol-day,
  both sides), and called out for reviewers.
- **Risk:** study harness config leakage across arms. **Mitigation:** fresh `Config()`
  per arm; `restrict_symbols` copies are not relied on.

## Half 1 — Result: the evidence says DO NOT ARM

At 1.5 the gate does real risk work (34% of assignments avoided) but costs
