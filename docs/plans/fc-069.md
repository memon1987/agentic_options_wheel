## Group A — policy revivals (decide these first)

### Item 1 — `max_total_positions: 10` · pre-tag: **REVIVE** (enforce in `select_batch`)

**(a) What it is.** A global cap on concurrent option positions. `config/settings.yaml:54` (`10`), property `src/utils/config.py:316–318`, validation `:161–163`.

**(b) Verified status: no *enforcement* exists on the live path — but a *detective* check counts positions hourly and fails at >10.** Rev-2 correction (both reviewers): rev 1 said "nothing counts positions" — false. `tools/testing/regression_monitor.py` `check_risk_parameters` (`:817–829`) counts option positions and returns FAIL above 10, served at `/regression` (`cloud_run_server.py:1643`, **HTTP 500 on any FAIL**), invoked hourly at :45 market hours by Cloud Scheduler (`regression-hourly`, per `docs/releases/RELEASE_2026-04-07.md`; live-verify the job at build). That is a materially different premise: the choice is not "cap vs nothing" but "**preventive** cap vs **detective-only** (alarm an hour after the eleventh position opens, with no blocking)." Preventive consumers: `wheel_engine._can_open_new_positions` (`:255–257` — note it counted **short options only**, `qty < 0`) and `get_strategy_status` (`:597`) — both deleted by FC-068; `risk_manager.py:52/:307/:354` — zero-caller methods (card 7); `portfolio_tracker.py:401/:440` — CLI-only; `/config` `max_positions` (`cloud_run_server.py:1069`); stale k8s copy (`deployment.yaml:120`); `scripts/testing/test_trading_workflow.py:35`, `test_issue_audit.py:81–93` (card 16/§CLI). Read of `select_batch` (`execution_engine.py:460–599`) confirms its only limits are per-underlying dedup, the calls share ledger, and put buying power.

**(c) Recommendation: REVIVE.**
- *Strongest FOR:* buying power is the only *preventive* breadth limit on puts today, and it is a *collateral* constraint, not a *count* constraint — a volatile morning, a margin-setting change at Alpaca, or a universe/config change could legally deploy the whole account across many puts in one batch, and the detective check would report it an hour later, after the fills. A count cap is the blunt, cheap, last-line preventive guard, and the cheapest control on this inventory to put on the real path.
- *Strongest AGAINST:* in ten months of live trading, BP exhaustion and the effective universe (6 of 14 symbols, FC-048) have kept concurrent positions well under 10. The arithmetic says the cap may **never bind**: at today's ≈$103k portfolio, `max_position_size 0.35` sizing against put collateral exhausts BP at roughly **4–8 concurrent puts** depending on strikes — BP hits zero before the count hits 10. Reviving at 10 may be pure ceremony; if so, DELETE (keeping the detective check) is the consistent choice.
- **Cap VALUE is its own sub-decision (rev 2 — rev 1 left it unflagged).** Confirming REVIVE at `10` ratifies a number nobody ever chose. Build-time audit query (marked, feeds the value decision): max concurrent short option positions ever held, from `trades_from_activities` fills minus expiries/closes per day — if the historical max is 6, a cap of 10 guards nothing and the operator should set the number deliberately (historical max + headroom, or an explicit "10 is fine as a catastrophe bound" on the record).
- **The delivered guarantee is a *soft* cap — stated so it is signed with eyes open:** with the recommended counting (calls count toward N, covered calls never blocked), total option positions **can exceed N via call writes** on held shares. The precise contract: "no *new put* is selected when short option positions ≥ N." That is the intended shape — calls on owned shares reduce risk — but it means the detective check (card 16) must use the same semantics or it will false-alarm.
- **Blind spot, stated:** the count is positions-snapshot-based and **cannot see resting unfilled orders** — the same open-order window FC-068 §1 documents for the deleted stage-6 guard and FC-009 owns. Two `/run` cycles racing a slow fill could each pass the count. Not solved here; not solvable from positions data; on the record.
- **Alternative weighed (rev 2 — the card previously never considered it):** a **per-batch bound** (`max_new_positions_per_batch` — the semantic of the just-deleted `max_new_positions_per_cycle`, revived in `select_batch`) targets the actual catastrophe scenario ("one volatile morning deploys the whole account") *more directly* than a total-count cap, is immune to the resting-order blind spot (it bounds this batch's additions, not a racy global count), and needs no positions snapshot. Decision offered both ways: total-count cap (matches the knob being revived, guards slow accumulation too) / per-batch bound (guards the burst, simpler) / both (belt and braces, two small checks in the same function). Recommended: **total-count, plus the per-batch bound if the audit query shows bursty fill days** — argue at sign-off if the audit says otherwise.

**(d) Cost.**
- *REVIVE (S):* in `select_batch`, count open short `us_option` positions from the positions snapshot (already fetched when calls are present; put-only batches gain one `get_positions` call — or reuse `/run`'s existing snapshot) plus batch-selected so far; when `count ≥ max_total_positions`, drop with a new `DROP_MAX_TOTAL_POSITIONS` reason (extends the closed `DROP_REASONS` enum, flows into FC-068's `selection_dropped` tally and P4 decision records for free). Counting semantics match the dead check (short options only, `qty < 0`) — rev-2 correction: rev 1 said the dead check counted *all* option positions; it did not, so the recommended counting is *the same as* the original and only the blocking scope (puts-only vs all) differs. Tests: cap fires at boundary; mutation-checked. Card 16's check 1 syncs to the same semantics in the same PR.
- *DELETE (S):* knob + property + validation + `/config` field + detective check 1 (card 16) + the dead readers + CLI/script edits (§CLI surface).

**(e) Blast radius if deleted:** `/config`'s `max_positions` field (verified: no dashboard consumer); regression monitor check 1 (card 16 — must be deleted with it or it 500s against a policy that no longer exists); `tests/test_config.py:34`; `portfolio_tracker.py:401/:440` + `scripts/testing/*` (§CLI surface); k8s yaml (OQ-8).

**(f) Decision:** ☐ confirm REVIVE ☐ flip to DELETE — operator. If REVIVE: **value** (10 / historical-max+headroom / other: ___) — informed by the marked audit query; **shape** ☐ total-count ☐ per-batch ☐ both; **call counting** ☐ calls count, never blocked (recommended) ☐ strict-original (short-only count, all types blocked).
