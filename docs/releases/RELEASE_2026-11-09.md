## Key Decisions & Rationale
- **Covered-call trigger 1.00; wheel stays 0.98 pending FC-112.** Same mechanism was the operator's principle; the wheel's change waits for measured evidence.
- **FC-112 is one-sided and pre-registered.** 1.00 is the default; 0.98 keeps only if it is robustly better (≥ 6 of 7 symbols, ≥ 0.5 pp median, under three fill models). Seven correlated names in one year cannot support a symmetric test.
- **Sim fills at the placed limit (FC-116), not a haircut.** It models what the roller places; the resting-leg residual is measured per row rather than assumed away.
- **Diagnostics never sit between order actions (FC-120 PR-1).** A "logging only" change added network reads inside the uncovered window; reads now happen after placements and terminals and are capped at 2 s.
- **Sell ladder = first rung, then the floor; escalation rungs off by default.** Nine of nine observed floor fills hit the true bid within 0.2 s, so intermediate rungs bought nothing on paper and added exposure.
- **Re-price only after the roller's own cancel settles.** An order cancelled from the console, or expired at the close, is never re-priced.
- **OPRA / Algo Trader Plus before real money** (operator-signed). The free indicative feed is the root cause; the 0.10 buffer is paper-era mitigation.
- **No pushes to main during market hours.** Every push redeploys both bot services (FC-125).

## Discoveries
- Alpaca's `indicative` option feed returns modified quotes: off the tick grid, seconds old at placement, offset from the true book by up to 0.35 on the observed legs.
- A marketable one-lot fills at the real book within a second regardless of how far through the limit sits; a non-marketable one rests 20+ s or never fills.
- The order-path circuit breaker has never recorded a failure (dead `except RetryError` branch) — FC-131.
- The bot `/health` answers 200 even when `Config` fails to load, so an invalid profile deploys green — FC-132.
- `covered-call-engine` has no BigQuery log sink; its events live 30 days in Cloud Logging.
- The chain lake refuses a narrower strike window instead of widening, so AMD rebuilds from the vendor every Saturday (~450–490 s) — FC-133.
- `scenario_sweeps` is append-only with one row per status; any join must take the latest row per `run_id`.
- A profile's `min_net_credit_per_contract` is dollars per contract, not per share (a 100× error caught in plan review).
- Unpinned dependencies can turn main red with no code change (pyarrow 26 vs numpy<2, hotfix #139 by the parallel session).
